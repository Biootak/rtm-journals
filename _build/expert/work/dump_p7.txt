No worries,

Try checking the tick chart if possible to see the DP. You know it definitely exists as it broke the highs/supply. Don't forget that S/R doesn't always align perfectly as the DP is a kink which offsets it one way or another. IF explains this somewhere in a post, but I saved time and made a new screenshot instead of finding it.

So the only way to see the DP exactly in real-time is to be looking at a tick chart. However if we cannot check it; as the screenshot shows we assume the whole thing is a DP to break the highs and price can turn anywhere within this kink, so basically we wouldn't trade it if we didn't have a tick chart as our R:R would be bad.

In regards to economic news release, I believe its the other way around. The news helps price get to where SD is faster to help fill orders.
