Since I'm sitting here bored as my Broker has temporarily closed out the platform for maintenance, I spent some time to draw out a recent concept I've been working on. Here is my interpretation of what it looks like:

1. Find a Base-Pole-Flag: where the Flag portion forms in a HTF supply or demand zone (the HTF confirmation is very important)

2. Once price starts flagging, drop down to a lower time frame and start marking out the FL's and FTR's that price makes as it spends time in your HTF supply or demand zone.

3. Mark the important FL or FTR that supports price's move in to the HTF zone and marks the end of the 'Pole' and starts the FL (this is the important FL that you want to see price engulf before looking for a place to get short).

4. Price will move sideways for a while while it consolidates. Just be patient...wait for the engulf.

5. After the engulf has occurred, start looking for a place to get short.

6. Find the high limit of the FL that formed within the HTF supply zone and watch for the area in the FL where the last orders (most extreme orders) could possibly exist. This is the MPL (I think).

7. Wait for the deep retrace to this MPL and get short with a tight stop. These entries are the ones that can be 1:10 R:R or more.

I am not sure if my drawing is 100% accurate, but I will use this idea in my trading next week to refine my entries and find trading opportunities that have a higher R:R.
