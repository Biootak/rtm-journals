Hi Peter,

This is a relatively new concept for me, so hopefully a more experienced trader can step in and correct me if I am wrong...but here is my understanding of what I mean by 'source' engulf.

When price is reaching an important HTF supply or demand zone, I drop down to a lower time frame to see how price forms as it reaches in to the area of importance. On its way in to the zone, price will FL many times (base-pole-flag...etc). FTR's will also form & I watch for these as well. What I look for is the original FL or FTR that acts as the source of the major push up in to the HTF zone. I also look for major S/R levels to mark the FTR that forms after the break of the S/R level. Basically, I want to find the FL or FTR that is important...not just FL's that form as a result of the important FL's. It is critical to find out which FL/FTR is the origin of the move in to the HTF zone because it is this FL/FTR that matters when it comes to a possible. engulf.

Previously, I would be watching the charts and point out what I thought was an engulf, when in reality all I was looking at was price breaking the high/low of a reactionary FL or (meaning nothing of importance happened). Here is a illustration I made a few pages back in my journal that I think is correct (nobody corrected me on it) and it does a decent job of illustrating this point.

As you can see on the illustrated chart, the original FL/FTR (arrow 1) is the one that occurred on the very Left.This is the FL/FTR that formed after a break of an important S/R level...because of this, I would deem this FTR/FL as being important and the 'source/original' FL/FTR. You can see from this drawing that price attempts to break the original FL/FTR (arrow 2) but isn't able to. Look at Arrow 3: This arrow breaks the top limit of the RBD that formed at arrow 2 making it appear that an engulf has occurred when it hasn't.

The true engulf only occurs when price is able to break the top limit of the FL/FTR show at arrow #1. The engulf happens at arrow # 4. This is confirmation that the last orders have been taken from the initial source FL/FTR and price will move to the north after (hopefully) retracing to the origin of the engulf.

This is my best understanding of the concept, and although it is hard to put in to words, I hope this drawing helps. For anyone who reads this and finds flaws in my response to Peter: please let me know! I'd hate to lead another trader down the wrong path!

Hope this help Peter, please let me know if you still don't understand that concept and I'll draw a new diagram with more examples
