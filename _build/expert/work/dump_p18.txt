Regarding EUR-AUD: This is what I saw towards the end of last week which makes me think price is heading north.

Bodo - you have a good point about the wick that hit the QM level. It didn't EG the FL (shown by the dotted line) however I too wonder if that wick already picked up the buy orders that existed at that level.

The HTF (to me) looks bullish (to the level that Glory pointed out).

A QM is a price pattern, and they can fail just like any other type of PA pattern. The two key aspects that must be present for me to consider trading a QM is:

1. Valid EG of source/original FL

2. The QM is setting up in agreement with the higher time frame, and there are no HTF engulfs occurring in the opposite direction of the direction you plan to trade the QM (Ex. if you are going to trade a bearish QM, make sure there aren't any recent HTF engulfs to the upside).

I know this isn't much of a definitive answer to your original question, but hopefully it helps a little.
