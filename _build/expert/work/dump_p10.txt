Well yes, there are multiple engulfs, DPs, etc using multiple timeframes. That's where screen time comes in

Another thing, don't try to collect huge puzzle in 1 attempt. I.e. if you are studying engulfs then start with swing low and swing high engulfs. Forget the PAZ, CP, FL etc. Focus on 1 thing and learn it. There are definitely setups only with key ingredient -engulf of swing extreme (it applied to any RTM concept).

EJ trade was taken not too low. it was taken higher in HTF. Then your setups was very strong. Your targets were scalp level. So it worked out very fine since you traded within market conditions and within what price was doing. AUDUSD was a forced trade IMO.

Nonetheless, every trader needs to finds his/her own style. If you like scalps... good for you. Just take them in good locations.

Regarding HTF... HTF concept is relative IMO. I.e. If you are taking a trade on M30/H1 then HTF could be H4 and Daily. If you take a scalp on M1... not sure the Daily is so relevant. At least not at this stage. IF looks at everything from what I see therefore he gets great entries... but it's better to learn this stuff than trying to do what he does

On the last note, give yourself a break. Literally, don't look at charts. I can give you some studies but in short it boils down to this:

- Overload Brain (lots of new info, you are doing HM, market analysis etc) - Short Release (break from trading, so everything could fall into the right places in your mind. If you won't it does not matter matter how much you work - you will not be able to use the learned stuff in efficient way) - Amazing High. (That's when everything makes sense. All the concepts you studies just makes sense and you seem them everywhere.) - Recovery (the is crucial. Post-High you might feel depressed, like nothing makes sense, etc. That's natural way the brain deals with highs. You need a longer break so your brain regroups, chemistry is adjusted etc. In these stages usually I can't even look at charts... felt bad about it for years. Then you will feel ready again and start new cycle. That is the formula for great performance, mental or physical;-)
