##### hs_p12_292 HotRod paras:3
Hey hoeschec here are a few things to consider regarding your loss. We always have to be aware of what price is doing in all TFs. While price can certainly move up from the point you took your trade you should realize that price is coming down from supply, and the best place to buy is at a fresh demand FTR. No matter what TF you use for entry it should be backed up by and at HTF FL/FTR. Your trade was in "no mans land" on the HTF.

I certainly see your reason for the trade and it does look enticing. Price did engulf an FTR, but unfortunately it also immediately hit an important FL and the target. Its important because it is supply that engulfed and formed at the last demand. You found a place that would bounce price, but the move away from price tells you whether to keep it and discard it and try again at a better place. In this case price cp'd away which leaves no demand levels left to "guard" your entry. What you want to see is an engulf of the immediate supply/demand. Price ended up forming a QM against you. You can't see this in real time, but can see the environment in which one can form if you keep in mind all TFs and what FLs are in play on each.

This is my take.
