Hi masalif,

Not a stupid question. MPL stands for "Maximum Pain Level".

I am probably the wrong person to ask, as I am just now beginning to understand these concepts, but I'll tell you what I currently understand the MPL to be:

The MPL is the most extreme set of orders left in either of the two the Flag Limits encompassing a Flag. The Flag Limits are also FTR's and contain the true supply/demand. Price will often fake out to the MPL to grab orders to obtain maximum liquidity, so it's a good idea to learn how to draw the MPL's and anticipate a fakeout to these levels after an engulf has occurred in the opposite direction.

Just some drawing's about concepts I've been thinking about lately.....

Fakeouts:

Here is a HTF chart showing a typical QM setup. I circled three critical areas that I will detail in future pictures.

More detailed:

My explanation:

These are good places to look for a low risk high return trade. I've noticed Salivan takes these frequently. I think the whole setup essentially revolves around two major pieces of PA: a fakeout followed by an engulf within the scope of a QM. For a bullish setup, we look on the higher time frames for a recent fakeout (to the downside) followed by an engulf into a PAZ (above). The fakeout forms the LL portion of the QM. The HH portion of the QM is the engulf into a PAZ above. This engulf is only valuable if it hasn't hit it's target yet. An engulf that hits it's target before retracing wouldn't be useful.

Let's go back to the fakeout for second....the fakeout is where all of the detail begins for the actual entry when price returns later on. I notice there are three areas that matter within the scope price forming a fakeout:

1. The decision point where price breaks down to form the fakeout (this is an FTR, a DP, and a FL). 2. The engulf of the DP to FO (price breaks the upper level of the FL that acted as the DP for price to EG) 3. The FL that forms as a result of the engulf of the previous FL that acted as the DP for price to fakeout. This new FL is the true demand and origin of the move up. It is within this FL that price will bounce on return. The lowest level of this FL is the MPL and price will often fakeout to this extreme set of orders. We can anticipate this by setting an entry order with a very tight stop loss. Nice R:R.
