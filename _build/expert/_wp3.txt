##### warren_p3_61 2
Knowing yourself is such a key thing and I've learnt that for me, if I try to scalp/day trade I end up messing about with the order too much, I do far better taking trades on the hourly and above and then just leaving them and treating it as mechanically as possible. The other key is I use tight stops and try to enter as close to the zones as possible so if it does break the zone I'm trading from I'm out straight away. It means plenty of losses, (around 30-40% of my trades are losses) but my winning trades are always far bigger than the losses.
##### warren_p3_61 3
I would sum up my approach as purely statistical. I don't worry about whether any individual trade is a winner or a loser, the only thing I care about is whether I feel the trade I've entered has given me a statstical edge. The key factor for myself in the journey to becoming consistently profitable was getting to grips with the mental approach, not the technical approach (although that's of course hugely important as well). Learning to let my trades run to their targets was the key and the only way I could do that was to detatch myself from the individual trade results and look at the bigger picture, again that's pretty much what you've explained earlier on.
