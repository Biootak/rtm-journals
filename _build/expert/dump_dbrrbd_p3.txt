gilben,

You have a good eye and good analysis. As I tell most people, the first part of analysis is being anal, and your attention to detail is good. I will add on a few observations for you.

Above your first compression zone where the C letter is located you have two areas in blue boxes for buying. Technically correct, but dont loose sight of the higher time frames and where the current time frame is, and in this instance the Euro is winding down and the two buying areas wont have the same effect, they will very get blown through. The major selling areas higher up the chart tell you the story.

Compare the other compression zones and the effect of buyers had on the price when it came back down to test them. Virtually all of them provide little impact.

There are times when price runs off quickly such as prior to your 2nd last compression zone, the 2nd compression zone slowly down price and also setup a move back up to collect more sellers where you see your 2 decision areas way over on the left. This also was a resistance area of significance. Those deep pull backs are probably among the most difficult part of trading, in that they throw you for a loop until you can see where the next most obvious resistance is for a continued down move.

Good work!
