Hi EM, A FL occur occurs anytime a barrier is broken, retraces, and advances to hit an opposing TG. Ergo, it also Fails To Return. When price comes back in the opposite direction and takes out the FL, its once the MPL of the opposing FL is taken out that the new FL becomes a UFL or LFL (once it also hits its TG). Its Ping Pong back and forth.
