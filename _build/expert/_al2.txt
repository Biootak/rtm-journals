When price comes back in the opposite direction and takes out the FL, its once the MPL of the opposing FL is taken out that the new FL becomes a UFL or LFL (once it also hits its TG).   [2] Should Price have turned here? Which of course we can answer because FO's can be predicted.
  [3] The only time I use a cap RBD/DBR as an actual trade is if its the MPL/LS trick. Which just looks like a little hiccup (FL/kink) on a higher scale but looks like an extreme MPL on the entry scale
