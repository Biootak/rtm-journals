A weird homework that I am currently doing. Surely the answer to this phenomena lies in the concept of FLs being created and destroyed. This, for me, is the easiest way to spot FO levels and nice places to look for bounces.

I understand they are what we call LS levels. What I look for is price levels that was touched from above and below, preferably to the pip touches. Say we are going from down to up, price would break the SR up, dip, make a decision, then engulf local high (confirming the FTR) which eventually engulfs previous swing's high.

The decision point at the dip after the break of SR is tradeable on return. Preferably with CP, CPLQ, BSZ, or fake SD level approach.

The bounce could happen anywhere inside the FTR, most likely at the SR first, creating liquidity pools below them for ITs to enter at MPL as usual.

The entry would be clear using LTF analysis. Stops are always beyond the FL/FTR in the same scale.

Targets would be the next higher up FO SR (where tricks were played).

Also the usual disclaimer: I don't expect them to workall the time. The ones that fail would be learning opportunities Apologies if this post is a little weird. I want to record everything that might produce green pips
