I'm glad you asked.

How many times have you watched price react to something and gone, 'oh boy I wish I traded that when I noticed it..'? To be honest I didn't think for too long when I executed, I saw a setup (H1 selling) and I dropped down to the LTF and participated with a limit order, with reasons based on probabilities (more likely vs less likely) and potential expectancy (is there room to run!?).

I suspect you are a little interested in some deeper thought process, so let's try and 'unpack' what I might have thought in the moment.

Let's highlight reasons to justify it. What did I see? What boxes did I mentally try and tick off for this setup?

1. You have price consolidation (range) 2. It is consolidating in a 'good' spot on the HTF 3. With a break in the anticipated direction and an accompanying pole 4. This pole engulfs the bottom or top of another consolidated area (an FTR, a FL, a DBR, RBD, etc) (not at the time of entry.) 5. The entry is placed at some sort of FL, FTR, DBD/RBR that formed near where the break of the consolidated area happened (a retest)

So we can very quickly assess that I ticked off 1, 3 & 5. There is no need to think twice about entry. But let's address your potential concerns with what I have just presented:

You might argue that with criteria 2 that price/reaction wasn't at a good spot because it hadn't actually traded in the HTF supply zone, but have you looked at a weekly or a monthly chart lately, which way is price going? Maybe that doesn't help so much, so let's be practical... draw a line through 1.4250 and look at it on the daily...zoom it out a little, let's not split hairs about 5-6 pips, this is a GOOD spot for LTF PA to form, previous support becomes resistance. I didn't speculate on this, price signaled its intention when it was ready to go and I had a place to define a setup, with setting the risk and places to look for profit pending future PA.

Which leads us onto your other concern...that demand below. Why didn't I wait for criteria 4 to happen before looking for entry? Well, I already spotted profit potential (room to run!) before it did engulf the next barrier, and the most recent momentum was down as the H1 printed.. You've got to remember I have a tiny 6 pip stop and the next potential barrier is 24 pips away. 4:1 instant potential. As we now know it went on to run to 100+ pips, I did not know that would happen but I participated in the moment knowing the potential that existed and I treated it one engulf at a time.

As for your long setup, I think you took a very reactive trade or an ill informed one, you haven't given enough consideration to the new information right in-front of you, it just engulfed the last printed H4 demand. Not your A+ setup...Price is telling you a story, use everything it offers you...

I want you to have a think about something else: Probabilities...Accepting loses....Risk Reward...You MUST have this in the back of your mind when you trade. I had accepted at entry that I could very quickly get taken out with the next M1 candle, there's always the chance of that happening, but I would have persevered and kept on trading similar setups if they came up, as long as the initial macro setup was still intact, or until price either made a higher high, or I have exceeded my attempts. I would gladly lose 4-5 trades at 6 pips for a shot at an immediate 4:1 or in this case 15:1. But in reality it's never going to get to that many loses on one setup, because by 2-3 losses I should know that I am wrong or wake up to the fact that I am failing some very basic entry discipline! Where as some traders will trade 30+ pip stops for one chance and sweat it out for a few hours to make a lazy 2:1 or 3:1, and they might have a perfectly legitimate reason to do so, such as not being around to baby sit trades..But I think that is a very hard and slow way to make money.
