EN: ****CAVEAT**** Only my view of the terminology to avoid confusion *** ⏎  ⏎ Decision Points – Kink/Failure to Return (FTR)/Flag Limit (FL) ⏎  ⏎ I see the decision point as the pièces de résistance in the RTM arsenal. ⏎  Sometimes also referred to as a kink, FTR or Flag Limit, it represents ⏎ the decision made at a barrier in the past. For the sake of consistency ⏎ and to help alleviate any confusion I see it occurring in (3) stages ⏎ which are outlined below. ⏎  ⏎ Stage One - The Kink ⏎  ⏎ Price approaches a barrier such as minor Support/Resistance (S/R), Major ⏎  S/R, Left Shoulder, Ignored barrier or break in/out of Compression (CP) ⏎  Price pauses and begins a retrace or pull back once the barrier has ⏎ been broken. It is this hiccup that is now the kink. ⏎  ⏎ Stage Two – The FTR ⏎  ⏎ If price fails to retrace further and proceeds in the original direction ⏎  it forms a FTR once price crosses past the origin of the retrace, it ⏎ has failed to return. At this point a decision has been made by the IT’s ⏎  to absorb the remaining orders and likely set new orders to be used at a ⏎  later point. However it is not a confirmed flag limit. ⏎  ⏎ Stage Three – The Flag Limit ⏎  ⏎ The Flag Limit (FL) is confirmed when an opposing one is hit. Until it ⏎ is hit the kink and FTR still form part of a flag’s body. This means the ⏎  FTR and kink can retrace further prior to the opposing FL being hit. ⏎  ⏎ Decision Points- Visibility ⏎  ⏎ Its also important to understand the best DPs are those ones which are ⏎ invisible in the higher time frames. You have to remember that a DP ⏎ could form and be hidden within the range of a candle seconds after it ⏎ happens.
