OK here are some more breakdowns of the decisions. The realization already is that price is only ever moving from one decision point to another, and the invariant at any decision point (DP) is value. If price falls away from an area, its decided price is too expensive - this is the invariant at this level at this moment. The basis to that invariant is the amount of supply that such a DP attracts. After the decision is made, different market participants may then join in, not until, adding more supply and helping the invariant hold when price returns - RTM style trading does exactly that.
##### finddecision_p6_144 2
The core move that we see is a move up into a space we can reason about, which is, if upwards, an DP area where it was previously decided price was too expensive. There will probably be resting limit orders, as well as active traders watching as price returns to the area. Everyone is waiting for some confluence depending on their trading modality, but at some point the supply/demand invariant holds or it breaks. So the move up happens, and the sign that price is too expensive is the engulf down. Some engulfs matter more than others it seems, and if a key DP is engulfed, price ascends in the winds of demand that it consumed, until those winds fade out and price returns to that last place where supply was overwhelming. This is the FTB trade down. And of course, the key to the DP is how the decision was made - was it timid, or was there conviction.
