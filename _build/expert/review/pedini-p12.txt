### pedini_p12_4
EN: Heya Cas,

When drawing Compression PAZ's which is what I drew up on my gold chart,
 you want to be absolutely sure that price is going to go to the other
end of the compression. The only way to do this is to make sure the last
 pocket of supply or demand has been engulfed. Until this happens, we
don't know compression is going to work and really it can go on forever
(just look at AUDNZD).

Looking at your lines now:

1 - This was a test of a flip level, it formed the drop base rally at 4,
 but there is still supply above us (so its not the edge of our PAZ)

2 - Again even if this level is broken, which it was, there still remains to be supply above that needs to break.

Finally price breaks into our PAZ at 3. This was the last of the supply
till the other side of the zone and that is why it is our PAZ level. You
 can see price then retraced tested out Drop Base Rally at 4 and
continued through the zone.

I hope this helps,

Peds
