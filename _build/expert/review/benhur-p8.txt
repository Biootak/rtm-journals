### benhur_p8_11
EN: Hi VT

The moment when things clicked for me in my studies was the moment I realized nothing in the market is technical. Instead I started looking for places where I could find unfilled orders. Today, when I look at the chart I am searching for the ultimate bouncing levels, then I consider if I want to trade these levels.

At decision points there are unfilled orders and you can expect a bounce there. but be aware that a decision level is just the nearby barrier of a PAZ (price Action Zone). The further boundary of that PAZ can react too, because this is the first level that price was balanced with opposing orders, therefor just beyond it you can find unfilled orders. This is why you should consider FTR, FL, DP, CP, QM, DM - these all are variety of formations that unfold imbalanced order flow that you can use in your trading.

To make things more complex, yes you should drill down forever if possible to really fine your level. You should always remember that time is no factor of the market, therefore time frame is just a random necessity to document price. The events of the markets are documented by price only. So our job is harder to extract the finest level to act upon. Yes is no that straight forward, And yes we should be very happy it is like that, only the busy miners get reach the precious rocks.

To be more practical, do what I did for weeks during my initial learning phase; take two lines one for above price one for below market price. stare at one minute charts and place you lines where you know price will give a bounce. Do so for whole sessions with great patience and motivation to really see price in action. If you do so, come back and share with the RTM community your finding. I am sure there are going to be at least few priceless insights you take for life.

EN: I got this question in my PM, I think it worth to post my reply in public.

The question was related to my definition for Flag-Limits. as some of you know, I tend to breakdown everything to the OF arrays, by doing this I can assume where unfilled orders are located:Think of a Flag-Limit in terms of orderflow balance / imbalance.

The flag area show us where there was balanced OF, while the pole (the approach or the break) is where we assume to find imbalance.

We also assume that we can find unfilled orders where there was first (or last) imbalanced OF.

This, in theory explains the FL under and above.

سؤال مربوط به تعریف من از FL لیمیت‌ها بود. همان‌طور که بعضی‌هایتان می‌دانید، من همه چیز را به آرایه‌های OF تجزیه می‌کنم و با این کار می‌توانم حدس بزنم سفارش‌های پرنشده کجا قرار دارند: به یک FL لیمیت از منظر تعادل/عدم تعادل جریان سفارش فکر کنید.

ناحیهٔ فلگ به ما نشان می‌دهد کجا OF متعادل بوده، در حالی که میله (نزدیک‌شدن یا شکست) جایی است که فرض می‌کنیم عدم تعادل را پیدا می‌کنیم.

This is why you should consider FTR, FL, DP, CP, QM, DM - these all are variety of formations that unfold imbalanced order flow that you can use in your trading.

To make things more complex, yes you should drill down forever if possible to really fine your level. You should always remember that time is no factor of the market, therefore time frame is just a random necessity to document price. The events of the markets are documented by price only. So our job is harder to extract the finest level to act upon. Yes is no that straight forward, And yes we should be very happy it is like that, only the busy miners get reach the precious rocks.

To be more practical, do what I did for weeks during my initial learning phase; take two lines one for above price one for below market price. stare at one minute charts and place you lines where you know price will give a bounce. Do so for whole sessions with great patience and motivation to really see price in action. If you do so, come back and share with the RTM community your finding. I am sure there are going to be at least few priceless insights you take for life.
