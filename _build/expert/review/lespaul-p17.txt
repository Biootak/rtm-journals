I have some doubts about how you choose which level have to be engulfed in order to use it as confirmation.

Think in terms of Flag Limits: :Picture a Rally - Base - Rally Or Pole - FTR - Flagging if you like.

When price breaks the limit, it will likely fill the base.

Now look at what was engulfed in those charts and then look at the distance to the base.

If price engulfs the limit and retraces, entry is at DP to engulf, and target is the base of the pole.

Hope this helps.
