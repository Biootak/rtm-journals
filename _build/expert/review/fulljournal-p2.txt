Htf show us a zone where there are interest to sell /to buy.

Ltf show us a zone more precisely these zone.

we need to know the first trouble area where price take a decision.(LTF)

if you are a day trader you will close all position at first trouble area

if you are a long term trader you will check every trouble area the price decision. to the final target.(possible retracement)

If you take a trade to a flag limit (the best entry)these are stress free entry.

if you take a trade in the middle of flag you could suffer a retracement and stop loss too.

i think we need to know the limit that the price will not exceed up to the final target.(flag limit)
