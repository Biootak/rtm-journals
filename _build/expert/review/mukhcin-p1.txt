### mukhcin_p1_13
EN: Mukhcin wrote: a loss trade...i can't find a reason of FO. and is it and eng or FO...?

It seems like you got the direction right that it is going up.

FO areas are there to trick retailers. Just like what IF implied- when ITs want to sell, they need retailers to buy.

Not all FLs work and FTB is only for those FLs that work. I used to
think that breaking important areas create a strong bounce during FTB to
 the strong FL made. However, it doesn't seem to be the case. In my
opinion, the FL reliability doesn't depend on the importance of the area
 it broke, but on the importance of the area it is in. Of course, watch
for PA.

Try watching the RTMA lite videos and the other lengthy videos. They helped me a lot. :)

-EM

به‌نظر می‌رسد جهت را درست گرفتی که بالا می‌رود.

ناحیه‌های FO برای فریب خرده‌فروش‌ها هستند. همان‌طور که IF گفت، وقتی بازار می‌خواهد بفروشد، به خرده‌فروش‌ها نیاز دارد که بخرند.
