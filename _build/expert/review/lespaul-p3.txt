This can also be used for trend continuation. It is kind of quasimodo
within the trend. Works best if the the countertrend-swing bounced off
unimportant levels, so the probability to enter at the setup level is
the highest because the trend is always your friend ;-) But a trend also
 ends at some point, and most of the time this happens at HTF levels.

But since I also like to trade countertrend on HTF, it is a good method
to get in when an engulf happens at M30 or higher at a HTF supply or
demand zone. Thats a good sign that pro money is active and they want to
 retest a decision point to confirm the trend reversal. If you look
through your examples, you see that most of the time, a touch trade just
 doesn't make sense, because price ITSELF tells you time is right to
step on the train when it engulfs previous swing levels. So instead of
trying to catch tops and bottoms, it's better to wait for this setup to
happen so that we can enter on the retest of a broken level. Also
important. If you look through your examples, you see that the REAL move
 in the opposite direction happens AFTER the retest. So there is no
reason to be nervous to miss the move. Price itself will tell you when
it's time to change direction. This is something very important I have
learned in the last weeks and months. For me, there is only one level I
would enter with a pending order right now: If the level is a) a HTF
level, b) the turning point of a big swing, c) lots of time went past
and d) the level is fresh and has a clear decision point (S/R flip) to
enter at. Especially midlevels, such as DBD or RBR on HTF always need
this kind of setup confirmation first in order to trade the reversals. I
 just got stopped out too many times and this prevented me from growing
my account.
