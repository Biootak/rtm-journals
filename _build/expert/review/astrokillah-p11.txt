EN: Hi PinkPanter,

I only trade when I can babysit my position. Forex is designed to take
stops, I am not a superman like Master Traders here that can pin-pint
exact entry with 1 pip stop loss. I have given up trying to do that.

I normally trade when there is no red news. I'm a scalper, so when price
 ranges, it will either get to my TP or if it overshoot my 'mental
stop', it will always retrace and let me get out with some minor profit.
 And I like to enter a trade when I see existence of multiple barriers
behind my stop loss. Because I know even when my stop is hit, price will
 get to the next barrier and bounce back towards my entry, allowing me
to get out with minor loss. And more often than not, I split my
positions. So if my full risk is 5 lots, I will split them into 2x2.5
lots, I would enter with 2.5 lot first, if it goes into drawdown, I will
 add the other 2.5 at the next barrier. Most of the time, price would
bounce back towards my entry. This kind of trading can get ugly (so a
hard stop is required beyond the second position), but 90% of the time
it gives amazing return.

On one of my diagram above, I mentioned 'contrarian', this is crucial in
 my trading, it's about knowing where price wants to go (I call this
'juicy' level). Most of the time we focus so much on entry, but
contrarian is target-oriented, I can enter a trade without the best
entry, with a bigger stop loss than I normally use, but I feel good
about it, because I am 99% sure that price would get to where I think it
 will go. So before I take a trade, I need to ask myself, where is the
juiciest level I see on the chart? And where is a logical place for IT
to take stops before they go towards their target. If I see a juicy
level above current price, I would never short. Likewise, I will never
long if there is a juicy level below.

Lots of words, not sure if it helps. I guess what has really made a
difference in my trading is when I put my heart and soul into
understanding price movement on 1-min chart.
