EN: Its very difficult, if not impossible to give signals using RTM
reading way. Why? Because even the right level can be FOed. So, knowing
that, we always should have hard stops somewhere, but we don't wait for
the hard stop to be hit, instead, after price break our level (fail
level), we exit right at the next chance, when price bounce back to our
fail level so we can exit without loss. Now, imagine that every level
could have inside a few fresh orders. Would you wait always for MPLs
inside each levels? If yes, you will miss a lot of opportunities. Would
you wait lower than MPL? Maybe you wouldn't have the chance to exit at
BE if your level fails. So, you have to take quick decisions, even if
you have a good level and a good signal. At the same LSK IF can take a
BE or a small profit, but in the same time others can take losses.
Because of these multiple factors, its very difficult to do that. And
imagine how much pressure you can put on IF with that. However, i'm
planning to create a group of traders soon on the whatsapp, formed with
those who can participate at meetings daily and share our setups for
