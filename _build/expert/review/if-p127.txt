It made me realize something important that i missed.

I tried many times to spot engulfs into a FL (opposed to engulf of a
FL). But when doing that i was considering in the same way a break in
between two targets Inside the FL than a break in between the S/R kink

kink and reaching first iLS, price reaches it's TG and therefore
validates a new UFL

Any break of that UFL is actually PA UP... So in that situation we
shouldn't expect price to retrace to the UFL we came from and then get
further into the LFL...

Lesson learned.
