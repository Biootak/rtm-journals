### if_p48_14
EN: Shift the focus and importance away from trade results and focus on process.

Profit or Loss, if a trade is identified and setup properly, and
executed with proper risk management, thats a good trade. Even if it
loses money (shit happens?). But if it loses money, the focus should be
to dig deeper and see if a mistake was made or if it was a valid trade
as per the methodology taught in the program.

That will take away some of the trigger shyness. For every trader to
have 100+ winners in a row.... possible or not, the expectation can be
crippling. If you shift the focus to the process of trading,
irrespective of the results, it'll be easier for them to do what you've
taught them so well already.

Just my 2 cents

Cheers!
