### if_p21_14
EN: I got a PM, probably today, from a member who's been trying for many
years to trade well. He admits he's still not got it, and gave me a list
 of his ideas for my advice

This is good stuff, and I want to share it

If you would be kind enough to advise me if I am at least close to the Golden Egg it would be appreciated.

1: There is no such system that is a "Holy Grail" ...full stop.

Wrong! The market is run by people who know what they're doing. They use
 a finite number of tools to trick retailers into offering orders up to
them. They use a finite number of algorithms to do their work for them.
In order to avoid chaos, they have rules they abide by (honour amongst
thieves)

Once you learn these tricks, how to spot these algorithms, and the rules
 they obey, you will be in the mind of the market makers, and you can
trade just as they do

2: I have played with indicators until I have worn them out, they are lagging in price and can not either predict or work.

Absolutely! The Market Movers know these indicators too!

3: PA together with Supply and Demand is king.

PA at important S/D S/R levels is key. Price history tells you which are important

4: Money Management is a top priority. No one can out run, predict or me smarter than the market.

Money management IS key. But it's not difficult to out run, predict and be smarter than the market

5: Always respect higher time frames in respect of placing trades on lower time frames.

Very true

6: If an acceptable set up is seen calculate the risk to reward ratio (this should be 3:1 at a minimum)

3:1 is a very very low number, but it's OK to begin with

7: Before entering in any trade make sure there is a bullish or bearish candle confirmation present.

Forget about candles. It's engulfs that are important

8: Place stop losses accordingly and and move to break even at the earliest opportunity.

This is a massive subject. But in essence: If you're not at the screen,
do put your stop losses beyond true S or D. But know that price will so
very often retrace to pick up extra orders. This leads on to your next
problem - the retrace is there to take out your break even. If you fear
the BE hunt, get out at TG1 and take the trade there again if the setup
looks good

9: Patience is a virtue (one area I fail in badly)

Amen to that!!!!

10: I can not seem to stick to one style of system, this I find very frustrating and another of my bad traits.

The only style or system that works is the one used by the Market Movers. Learn it

11: Chart colours and set ups. (I
can not settle on on style, this really concerns me and do not
understand why I do it?) I am a visual person and suspect this may be
one reason?

Set up your charts just like mine, and don't change them. I like looking at charts with my template And don't forget the Readthe Market.com logo at the bottom
