Like Pedini said, for us, the H4 supply above was just a level, where we
 expected price to show us something to trade. So the price came in,
made a nice RBD, but this is not enough if it does not engulf the last
swing low. If it would have stopped above the last swing low, we would
not trade it, since there can still be HHs forming. An engulf of swing
low is the PA which says to us, it is relatively safe taking the trade,
given somebody who has the might to make an engulf is selling here.

Once we have an engulf at a significant level, all we need to do is find
 a nice place to leave your order and let it do its thing.
