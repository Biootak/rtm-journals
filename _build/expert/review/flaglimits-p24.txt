### flaglimits_p24_584
EN: akifusenet,

You're asking some very good questions which I was asking myself a month or so ago!! I don't have all the solutions but hopefully what I've learned is of some help. First, about the significance of flag limits...some of them are more "localized" flag limits. For example, FL1 is an engulf of a local high, while FL2 is more of an engulf of the flag area to the left (where we see price meandering around without any new poles). To me that tells me FL2 is probably the more significant than FL1 since we're engulfing a larger zone. FL3 is another story of price engulfing a smaller flag area, but it did work nicely several times as you see.

Now onto the more difficult question which I've been searching for as well...FL2 - was it engulfed or not? I don't think so. First, I'm not entirely sure that FL2 is the actual flag limit or not. On the M1 chart where you can't zoom much farther unless you have a tick chart, it's possible that the actual FL extends closer to the "SR" area. If it doesn't and price did move below it, I still see this as an FTR which would show up on higher time frames as a FL in its own right (they are the same thing). Note how price broke above the S/R, tried to get back in, and then moved above the original breakout high, confirming an FTR. Where would I place the stop? Well, I wouldn't have been completely comfortable with that original FL2 zone for previously mentioned reasons, but if I were, I would have placed it a pip or so below the base of it given this is an M1 chart. If I were stopped out, I would have probably given the FTR a try as well since it's valid too. Others might put it beyond the S/R but that isn't something I care to do. Note how in your bottom center graphic that FL2 may have been exceeded by a pip or so but if you use the FTR area it would have stayed within bounds.

Significance of FL/FTR has nothing to do with time but with what kind of flag area was engulfed. Are we looking at a strong engulf of a huge daily flag area or are we looking at price engulfing a local flag on the M1? That's what makes the difference in my experience.

Keep in mind this is just my two cents, but I hope it helps. This is an area of interest for me as well.
