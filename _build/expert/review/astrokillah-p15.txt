There is really no magic to my scalping. Price move from FL to FL, or
from Supply to Demand (vice versa). I read somewhere (I forgot where),
the best way to learn how to scalp is:

1) Open up 1-min chart

2) Use horizontal line only, draw lines at price levels where you think price will bounce

3) Watch as price hits your line, see how many times you got it right

4) The more you practice, the more accurate your prediction will be

A good place to start (in RTM) is perhaps the 'Finding The Decision" thread (under homework section).
