definition of a flag is when the top and bottom are liquidity gaps. In
other words you find the area on the bottom that was broken with strong
demand and the area on the top that was broken with strong demand. The
area in the middle is your flag or CP. Right? My flags on here are
marked with blue rectangles. The red lines are the extremes that we
would look for price action and the middle is where we don't want to
diddle! In other words there is either really clean ignored/broken SD

that I marked with trend lines are not flags. These areas are just CP,
thats why price moved through them. I don't know however why price
stopped at the yellow line. That demand was spiked.

If you look at the flag at the top with the red arrow, that is where I

 the engulfing. This means that I would look at the red lines of that
flag on lower time frames for a sign. This could be a swap level, QM,
