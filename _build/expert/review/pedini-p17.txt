Hiya man, no trouble at all!

1. This is a really good question, and yes the supply level you marked
is clean supply. There are however plenty of supply/demand traders
waiting there to short as well. If I had a bucket load of money to put
into the market, and I knew where the majority of the crowd want to
short I can push price higher, take their stops, and turn price at the
last possible chance. Was I granted to get my fill, no. But I knew it
was the safest short I could take.

2. The 1, 2, 3 is the "three drive pattern" (there is an article about
it in Markepedia). The important thing here is how price has an
explosive move up, then tests demand before moving up again. Demand is
being consumed on the way up, for an easy return journey.
