### if_p97_6
EN: Staurum, try to make it simple. For example, think about FL as a
retracement in price. How do you know when that retracement is over?
Answer, it is over when price hits the next FL to its left. That is
where orders are left waiting there to be filled (in the next FL).
After it hits the FL to its left (where orders are left waiting to be
filled), then you can say, the retracement (FL) is confirmed and we can
now draw a box around it. And we only look for FLs that are
significant. The significant ones are the ones that engulfed a FL to
its left. Otherwise it is not important.
FA: Staurum، ساده‌اش کن. مثلاً FL را مثل یک اصلاح در قیمت در نظر بگیر. از کجا می‌فهمی آن اصلاح تمام شده؟ جواب: وقتی تمام می‌شود که قیمت به FL بعدی در سمت چپش برسد. همان‌جایی که سفارش‌ها منتظر پر شدن مانده‌اند (در FL بعدی). بعد از این‌که به FL سمت چپش رسید (جایی که سفارش‌ها در انتظار پر شدن‌اند)، می‌توانی بگویی اصلاح (FL) تأیید شد و حالا می‌توانیم دورش باکس بکشیم. و ما فقط دنبال FL‌های مهم می‌گردیم. مهم‌ها همان‌هایی‌اند که FL سمت چپشان را اینگالف کرده‌اند. وگرنه مهم نیست.

Actually what we learn overall in this great forum is to read Price Action, and take advantage from that Edge.

And what is Price Action ?

Or a Price Action Zone ?

It is Price moving in a way to "prepare" a later decision.

For this reason Price Action is what happens between two DP.

In a CP PAZ, for example, Price can decide to go back and fill it or shoot in the opposite direction.

Also in this case the two extremes of this zone will be important on return.

At those points Price will either bounce or show a new Decision: EG

The Engulf tells us that Price violated "Structure" and changed "Trend" until the next Structure level to be retested.

The next DP

It is like Trend Structure. Bullish, Bearish or Sideway.

A trend is not anymore such, as soon as its structure gets violated.

A PAZ is completed as soon as it achieves its next Limit (DP or FL) and confirms or negate the move to the next one.

That's my "Thinking about Price".
