EN: Next up is USDJPY

Looking at the Monthly chart, I saw price is inside zone (A).

It's not my intention to introduce new strategy, but just to share what I
 have learned elsewhere. Zone (A) is called a 'Breaker' by ICT Traders,
and to them this is the place where place is most likely to bounce. A
'breaker' is a zone that has accomplished the following:

1) It is the DP that broke an opposing zone (B), but this is a fake-out as price quickly reversed back into the DP.

2) As price reversed and returned to this Breaker for the first time, it
 was completely ignored and price pierced through it easily, so in RTM's
 terminology, this is an Ignored level.

3) Now, when price return to it again from the other end, it shall become a high odd area to look for price bounce.

So I am eager to verify this theory. Now let's look at Weekly Chart:

The first thing I noticed is a possible flip area (or swap zone), and
also the double bottom (red circles) which is prone to fake-out. So I
wouldn't be surprised if USDJPY continues to drop until it hit 107.9.
