### sniffing_p7_3
EN: MCFly wrote: I'm not sure I like the explaination in the vids about why the gaps tend to get closed but in the end they mostly do.

Everyone points at S/D, Balance and Imbalance in a different way without explaining the real mechanics behind it.

Although in my previous posts I tried to make it clear why LQ gap gets filled but I’ll repeat it in a simple phrase;

In LTF LQ gaps gets filled because - When pro money enter with their big orders it moves the price away and against their entry price, therefore they await for price to return to their entry price to continue adding their orders.

In HTF LQ gaps get filled because – When pro money closes their buy orders they become sellers, and when they close their sell orders they become buyers.

Thank you.
