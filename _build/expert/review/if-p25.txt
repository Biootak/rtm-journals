Hi Buddy, "I understand that an engulf involves price making a lower
low on reaction to Supply, or higher high on reaction to Demand" - This
can be correct, but it certainly is not always correct, actually most of
 the time it isn't correct. You need to find the important engulfs of
flags, it is when these happen that an engulf becomes meaningful. Simply
 making a higher high or lower low will not show you any intent.

Also understand that an engulf will achieve the same thing as a FO (in
terms of retailer trading behaviour) but it is the signal that price
wants to go to the next important level in the direction of the engulf. A
 FO is simply a ploy to trap traders once the signal engulf has already
happened.

Study up on flag limits, there has been some very good posting on them
recently, understanding the FL and the engulfs of the FL's along with a
HTF analysis and you will be able to tell the FO from the Engulf.

Sorry if that was a little bit of a mouthful,
