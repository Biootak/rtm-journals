The number of trades is much higher than a number of setups I traded. It

 realize it - then I close trades to avoid further losses. Overtrading

I don't know if there is a hope in trading career for people like me,

Another big issue is entering after a reaction to a level - RR is worse
and bounce already happened - pointless. I'll work on that as well and
I'll post updates on my progress in this journal.

Where do I look for trades?

What I aim to do is to take touch trades in the direction of the trend.
But by trend, I mean situation when the price is coming from a valid
zone and didn't reach the target yet. I basically try to find places for
 a touch trade after continuation engulfs. I do this when ITs have
positions open and add on retraces by stopping traders out. When I see
that price is reacting to valid target and there is some kind of
reversal I assume ITs are short (for example) and then I look for
opportunities to join the move. So even though most of my trades can be
classified as continuation trades, all of them are reversal trades at
the same time - because if I sell, I sell against strong (usually)
bullish move on smaller scale, and vice versa.

It is important to analyze all scales and to make sure that you are not
trading against direction signaled by engulf on a higher scale.

I'll quickly mark EUR/USD charts to show what I mean:

This quasimodo is beautiful you can see how clever ITs are. Look how
long price was going up. As price moves up more and more people are
biased long and then big push happens. The way I think about this is as
follows: ITs don't have to do much here. People are biased long and ITs
"add fuel to the fire" to make the move look strong/scary/exciting,
whatever. The point is that now price is where they want to sell at and
people keep buying this thinking that price will go up forever . Btw. there is a big daily "marubozu" candle after pin bar - some people consider this an excellent buy signal.

Countertrend moves are usually strong and it may be counter-intuitive to
 trade against but that is what we do. Here is nice examples of moves
like that. I marked them with arrows. Red lines show where people got
stopped out recently. So I think that ITs are still short and there is
potential for continuation as there is a lot of space below.

Of course, it is easy to mark this stuff in hindsight - much harder to
do it real time because it often seems that move is genuine and it will
stop you out, but you need to pay attention to bigger picture and trade
against moves like these fearlessly.

 these positions. They don't want drawdown, so they will protect this
level by adding shorts and pushing price down.

retrace? You can see on my chart that there is no obvious better place
