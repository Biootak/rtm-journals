### frosty_p11_20
EN: Hi all, An FL is created when an important barrier is broken. This so-called important barrier can be any of the following: opposing FL, important SR, PCP, end of CP, and QM level.

When it comes to classifying the scale of an FL, we really only need to use two criteria: 1) It's accomplishment - what time scale does the broken barrier belong to?

2) It's visibility - On what time scale is the FL visible?

Now, I see many people still get confused on the issue of scale. When we call something an H4 FL, what do we mean by that, what is an H4 FL? I have not yet come across a definitely guideline on this. Some people call it an H4 FL because it is visible on H4 time frame, but I have recently ask Master IF about this, and he seemed to imply that it's not about visibility, it's more about its accomplishment: So let's say we have a clear swing high/low on H4 being broken/engulfed by a long extended candle (a pole) on H4 timeframe, so we are not going to see a kink on that long candle, but we will find a kink on 1-min chart that coincide with the swing high/low, so this kink visible only on 1-min chart, is actually a H4 kink. And if you can recall, IF has said it several times that we absolutely must go down to the lowest possible timeframe to find our kink.

However, a subsequent post by Master IF confused me Because he seemed to imply in the above post that visibility is what we should use to classify the scale of an FL.

Hence, the quest for the ultimate truth is still on-going....

cheers AK
