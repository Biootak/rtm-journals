of orderflow where price moves to find new equilibrium. When we are
talking about a spike to supply, there was more demand than supply once
IT pulled their orders. This allows the sharp move higher to strong
supply or an area with lots of retail buy stops (because IT takes
opposite of retail). Then price falls because of overwhelming supply.
