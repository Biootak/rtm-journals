EN: Hey friend I'm glad to see you post your homework, it seems you're even going faster than me, and you're doing a great job

To answer your question, I think Compression doesn't tell us the way market is going to go once it touches the next FTR, It can simply ignore that FTR and go to the next. What we know for sure, is that all buy orders (in bull CP example) have been cleared so when price breaks back into that compression, it will be very likely to see price going through in short amount of time.
