Don't forget that price already bounced from HTF demand here and we saw sign of buying off demand.

There are 2 types of engulfing that I'm aware off, Engulfing candles and Engulfing of a zone/Flag/Supply-Demand

Engulfings of zones are very powerfull, they happen for different reasons:

1) To consume the remaining supply (short orders) or demand (long orders)

2) Clear the path for the future, like Red used to say, Price thinks ahead of time

3) Fake traders out and get them envolved in a wrong direction and take their stops.

As you can see from the example below, price engulfed the Supply and price came back to a demand level within the range.

همان‌طور که در مثال زیر می‌بینی، قیمت عرضه را اینگالف کرد و بعد به یک سطح تقاضا داخل همان رنج برگشت.
