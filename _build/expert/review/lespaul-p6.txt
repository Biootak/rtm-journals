that the way I do it is right cause I am still working hard on it. I

much harder.

SCREEN 1

In the SCREEN 1 you can see that price makes low, turns, engulfs FL
(dotted line) and makes FTR. Then it starts travelling. north. The newly
 formed FTR is now lower edge of my PAZ.

Above, you can see an FTR that was mede on the previous way down. This
upper FTR is my top edge of PAZ. Will price bounce or go through? Only
PA in LTF can tell me.

SCREEN 2

In SCREEN 2 you can see the price shot through the FTR, which means that
 its supply becomes demand. It is now my lower edge of the new PAZ. As
far as the upper edge is concerned, it is formed by the nex FTR in line.
 What is PA going to do now? I don't know, I must wait and see.

SCREEN 3

In SCREEN 3 we can see that PA raced to the upper edge, engulfed it a
bit and got back into the PAZ. The bias is long after 2 engulfed levels
in a couple of minutes. PA starts flagging.

In this situation I took the failed EU trade. Had I done this analysis before? Well, no...
 If I had done it, I would have never taken the trade in the place I
did. In M5 it can be nicely seen that PA engulfed Flag Limit and
retraced to the origin of the move - upper edge of PAZ. Now it was
apparent that price wanted to go down to that SUPPLY TURNED DEMAND level
 (lower edge of PAZ). In my opinion this was the moment for taking a
scalp south.

But I was not aware of the situation. Due to my stupidity I did not
check the overal situation and took that trade - in the middle of that
flag / range / whatever we call it. It was nobody's land - no trade
