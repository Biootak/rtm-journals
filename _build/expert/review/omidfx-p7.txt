A Note before you begin

To all new traders:

This is a business, a very hard business.Every day we stare into the

account , trying out any system which was advertised well enough. We
found out that the market could chew us up and spit us out without
giving us a thought. We certainly paid our dues to that which would
provide for us later.

We imagined that we'd get rich quick, and that just didn't happen so easily.

What we had to do was put away all those silly indicators that promised
to give us an edge, and just look at what the charts were showing us.It
took thousands of hours of hard work and dedication to become successful
 traders.

to do next. We began to notice many different patterns and price
structures which indicate that price should move to here or there.

Trading live is a world away from trading demo. The emotions you will

those silly systems you left behind.

markets. They take it from PEOPLE. They take it from the 95% of people
