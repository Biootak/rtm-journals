based trade:

1. UJ trade: looking at the 4H-TF we see that in june price engulfed a
nice supply by a clean bullbar and after the engulf price made a very
nice FTR just above the engulfed supply, we now have stacked demands ie.
 a nice beartrap configuration. The outcome was as already mentioned by
 Les_Paul, indeed a FO /engulf PA based trade on the LTF

2. EJ-trade: looking at the 6H-TF we see that in june price engulfed a
nice supply, after the engulf price made a very nice FTR, its here were
price reacted yesterday and if you switch to the LTF you'll see an
engulf PA based trade. The engulf trade Myst mentioned wasn't valid in
my opinion since price didn't reacted at a significant HTF demand, that
demand was somewhat lower
