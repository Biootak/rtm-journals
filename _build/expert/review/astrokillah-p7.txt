kink you saw on the right shoulder, is often a strong supply zone. I
want to see price drop some significant distance below this kink,
because this will prove to me that this kink is the 'origin' of the down
 move. And hopefully within this kink, we will find a nice, clean DP
that has never been touched before. So when price return to this clean
DP, it will often hold and push price down again. Then it's up to us to
manage our trade properly, do not assume price will travel far, all we
can be sure of is, price is likely to travel to the next opposing kink.

immediately bouncing up. So I wouldn't touch trade there at all. But if
price plunged, breaking the blue and the pink FLs below, and then
