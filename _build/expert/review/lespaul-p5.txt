### lespaul_p5_15
EN: Hi Les_Paul and Hotochin,

I just wanted to add something which I dont think is always obvious.
When price breaks out and then retraces but holds under new demand that
has formed, out from the top of the PAZ, like the first example in the
chart LeS_Paul put togther, thats the failure-to-return - it tells us it
 couldnt return down because sitting here is fresh demand. When price
breaks out of PAZ 2, there is a clear RBR - the B is the FL, and then
price retraces, only for a bar. This bounces right above the FL - this
is the FTR as far as I understand it, because its major purpose for
being identified as a concept on the chart, is that it is a marker that
supply or demand is now imbalanced in the opposing direction - it is
another place that allows us to reason about order flow. And more
subtely, to me an FTR is the marker that price failed to get back to the
 origin of the move when it retraced, because of order flow.

I am sure you understand this actually, but thought maybe using some different verbiage might add to it.

MF.
