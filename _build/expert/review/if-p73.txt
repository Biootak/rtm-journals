### if_p73_6
EN: When we have an If/Then setup, we don't need PA for a RT entry. We
simply need to decide whether or not to wait for the very best price on
RT.

If it hits straight away, we have the very best possible R. If not, we
may miss decent decent swings within the forming flag while we wait.
