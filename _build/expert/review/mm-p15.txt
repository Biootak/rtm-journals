My opinion after checking your chart below: - the right H isn't in the same or lower line of price like the left one (starting of QM) - also the wicks are not so long - should be more obvious that there is rejection of the price at that level The teachers will tell - but I wouldn't consider this as valid QM.

We can get into trouble looking for a "to the pip" entry on a QM. In a QM at S, sure we look for the break above the initial H, which is where the buyers get trapped, but it's the DP to break back down after the HH that we look for in a QM, which need not be exactly at the level of the left shoulder
