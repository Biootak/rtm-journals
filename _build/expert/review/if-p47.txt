EN: Hi J9,

Sure. At point 1 on the down side, we have an engulf. The little flag on
 the left side is a decision, a decision to go higher and go back into
the M15 flag. On the way down, when this level marked by the red line in
 broken, we can say the demand was engulfed. Next price flags. It
bottoms, reverses back to the red line, then on the next wave down, it
makes a lower low. To me, that is a clear signal that price wants to go
lower. So, when price comes back to 3, to test the cap at 1, it is a
good place to go short.

And a point to clarify about QM. They do work in all timeframes, M1 up
to MON1. What I was referring to is their respective locations. We are
more interested in QM that form in historical S and D zones. So, the way
 you marked up your QM, was for a short. But I don't see any Supply zone
 there. There is supply for sure but the way I learned QM was to expect
them at higher TF supply zones (Daily Supply for example for shorts).
Perhaps the easiest is to go back to the QM article and read it again as
 that is probably clearer than my explanation.
