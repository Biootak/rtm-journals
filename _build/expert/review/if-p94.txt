The market is an herd, putting in cash.

The cash goes straight back to the people who created it out of nothing and loaned to them in the first place!

So, to understand the market is to understand the herd (of cash cows, in this case).

Can anybody in the herd understand the market?

People who understand the herd, leave the herd, so the answer is "yes, but only for a split second!"

So, if you want to know whether you'll be a great trader, simply ask yourself:

Do I believe in gods?

Do I believe in governments?

Do I believe in royalty?

Do I believe the news?

Do we have an emperor?

If the answer to more than one of these questions is "yes", then you're not ready to understand the market
