by IF:

Fakeouts are extremely easy to predict. Most SD traders still look
directly left, which is the big shortcoming of Supply/Demand trading.
It's further left again we should be looking.

Say price is coming up to a supply. The only way we can be sure that
supply will hold price down is if that supply has already visited the
extreme set of orders in the flag limit to its left. If the supply fell
short of that, we can anticipate a FO to those extreme orders. Extremely
 simple stuff.

We always look to what S or D are reactions to, rather than just S or D themselves.
