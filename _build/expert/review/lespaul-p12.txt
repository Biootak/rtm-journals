I hope you don't mind me giving my view on this buddy. What I think
Harry means when he says that, is, take for instance a textbook engulf
setup, price is heading to supply, price on the LTFs engulf an important
 low indicating bearish intention and then take the RT to a cap or FTR,

setup and study it till u know it inside out, the you will understand
what Harry means mate.
