EN: One thing I realised, when I looked at other traders journals
(specially recently omidfxt and salivan), one should not use HTF FL/FTR
or whatever to finetune the entry point. First locate the zone where you
 want to look for trade using HTF FLs/FTR or whatever. It gives a rough
idea about the location where you want to trade. Once you know the HTF
zone where you want to trade, then you can go to LTF and finetune the
entry for max R:R.
