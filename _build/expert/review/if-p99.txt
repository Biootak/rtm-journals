 orders with the SL above the entire UFL or LFL being reacted to.

require PA (engulf into space) and the BSZ TG needs to be at the kink of
 the 2DT or 2DB which is often in the body of the flag.

UFL/LFL being engulfed into space and the TG is the opposing FL.

-	More frequent than the trades based on EG PA

-	Tend to have less R than trades based on EG PA and;

-	The win rate may be slightly lower than trades based on EG PA

Only my two cents. Usual caveats apply

I recall IF answering another student about the same question you had on
 "I have a very very hard time finding space, engulf not hitting its
target.". Yes, we need to find space otherwise, there is no trade. If
it was easy to find space, then everyone would become a successful
trader overnight. The ITs will not make it easy and obvious.

Trading is based on your preference and your comfort zone and your
confidence in your analysis. There are master traders who take touch
trades and take confirmation trade. It all depends on the trade itself.
 First, I would suggest you get comfortable with confirmation trades
before jumping to touch trade. Confirmation trades will provide you
more winners and put money in the bank for you. It will also build
your confidence. Touch trading can be tricky, so you need to read the
market correctly.... . You need to know if the level it is reacting to
is the true supply..... you need to know how it reacted when it reached
that level....., for example did price impulsive move away from that
level and engulf an important FL to its left without hitting TG. If so,
 you have PA.. Now you want to see how price is retracing and
approaching your entry point.(in this example, I am describing a
QM)...... is it compressing, is it compressing and then a final
impulsive move to your entry level....These are the questions that you
need to answer. The 1/2 page on article on price reading in Marketpedia
speaks it all. So, I would recommend you reading it over and over again
 until you truly understand. If you have any question, then you can
post them and other students will help answer.
