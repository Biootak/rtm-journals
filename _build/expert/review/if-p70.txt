The short answer to your question is Demand exceeded Supply at a level you have not drawn. My observations in black.

Some general feedback using the chart for illustration:

Be cautious with creating too higher expectation from one engulf or one observation
 you make. Trading requires consideration of multiple sources of
information which may come from other time frames as well.

Be careful not to expect price to make higher highs or lower lows, just
because you identified an engulf of a level (note obstacle 1 & 2).

Pay special attention to the QM level drawn on the chart, the level is
more than a singular horizontal line, if you want to use just a single
line, draw it under the lows of the "1" and "2".

The QM level held price up on 4 occasions! On the 2nd, 3rd and 4th
occasion it made a higher high on this timeframe. There was no engulf of
 this zone, therefore you cannot rely on your expectation that price
would go any lower than this zone at any stage of your analysis.

In fact if you look on from point "4" the simple observation that no
supply was holding price down and new demand was being created would
tell you that a sell off was highly unlikely.

Also last but not least, let's just say you did enter a touch trade
short based on your assertion that price engulfed the low you have
identified, you have obstacle #1 immediately below you, reducing your
probability of success.

Hope this helps.
