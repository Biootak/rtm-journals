### jurij_p14_9
EN: Booking a loss on the first try after engulf signal is sometimes great
 because it allows for better places to enter with better RR.

This is what I call second chance entries. If the first try is a loss
and price goes to a better price before continuing in the right
direction then I give it another try (2nd chance) if this one fails too,
 then probably the read was wrong. Those entries allows to handle
extended FO that happen occasionally, especially if there are better
levels nearby.

AUDUSD

1. The signal

2. The loss on first try

3. The extended FO

4. Sniper entry on LTF PA

5. Price went in the intended direction after a massive trick
