WHAT IS AN ENGULF ?

The most important question in RTM training camp. First of all I want to describe a hypothetical battle.

Suppose that an army is advancing to conquer the enemy positions. Enemy
positions include a number of castles that formed a fortress. To conquer
 a fortress its FLAG should be conquer. As soon as the flag of first
castle was removed we must ask ourselves a few questions.

1-

What is the position of the conquered castle?

2-

Where they come from and where are they now?

3-

Is there a nearby castle, which is already under attack?

4-

After the conquest of the castle and down the old flag, did the attackers have raised their flag?
