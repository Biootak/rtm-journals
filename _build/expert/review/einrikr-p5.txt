However, what have I learned from this trade:

Targets

Setting a final TG for yourself is a good thing, so you know what you’re
 working up to. Watching it reaching your target helps building trust in
 your reads.

Do not however, neglect mid-range targets as they are very capable of
bouncing price. For example, price bounced off of 2 PCP points and got
as low as the cap of my point of entry.

It was a fun exercise though, getting in to a short, down to the PO I’ve
 set for a long and seeing price hit exactly that and turn around. There
 was 0.7 pip drawdown on this trade.

Expectations

50R (high of first day) or 70R (high of second day) is more than enough. Just be happy with it and take it.

I’ve been waiting for a bit higher R for 3 days now and it bugs me. It’s
 really annoying to see price going up and down and not hitting your TG.
 Sure, it didn’t hit my stop but the bit of extra R is not worth the
wait. Not for me it isn’t, not at this point in my trading career at
