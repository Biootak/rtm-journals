I think you theory about students "not being machines" was proven long
time ago with "The Turtles" experiment in stock trading. Where although
given strict step-by-step instructions how to trade profitably, by some
famous trader, majority of them was not able to follow it and really
trade profitably. Then the question WHY? comes to mind.

And the answer is rather simple, people have encoded in them various personality patterns and this is one of them.

So there are "procedure" people who have absolutely no problem following
 instructions and get things done and "options" people who just simply
cannot follow instructions and have to either create their own procedure
 or at least modify the one they have...which in trading with proven
system is rather fatal....On the other hand "procedure" folks have
terribly hard time creating procedures or they cannot create them at
all.. Just a small psychology lesson
