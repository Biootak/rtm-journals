This is what I think ---

1. Use the s/d, s/r flip, ftr, etc. to identify the potential and high probability price reversal regions in a HTF.

2. As price returns to these identified zones, we switch to a LTF to
see how price reacts. In the selected LTF, after price trades to the
zone from Step 1, we wait for price to go back down or up to engulf a
significant demand or supply formed before price reached the zone, or
use the various other reaction price patterns discussed to confirm IT's
intention of taking price down or up.

3. Then, as price returns to a supply or demand formed at or near the
zone identified in Step 1, a touch trade or confirmed trade can be
taken.

4. Place the stop beyond the edge of the zone identified in Step 1;
target 1 is the next demand or supply in the LTF used, while the final
target is the original of the move to the zone identified in Step 1,
often the next demand or supply in that HTF used.

The reason you wanted us to put the puzzle pieces together instead of
handing over the golden keys is for us to accumulate the chart
experiences (screen hours), and figure out what HTF and LTF would work,
and to form the psychology to follow the plan.

This is what I think ---

1. Use the s/d, s/r flip, ftr, etc. to identify the potential and high probability price reversal regions in a HTF.

2. As price returns to these identified zones, we switch to a LTF to
see how price reacts. In the selected LTF, after price trades to the
zone from Step 1, we wait for price to go back down or up to engulf a
significant demand or supply formed before price reached the zone, or
use the various other reaction price patterns discussed to confirm IT's
intention of taking price down or up.

3. Then, as price returns to a supply or demand formed at or near the
zone identified in Step 1, a touch trade or confirmed trade can be
taken.

4. Place the stop beyond the edge of the zone identified in Step 1;
target 1 is the next demand or supply in the LTF used, while the final
target is the original of the move to the zone identified in Step 1,
often the next demand or supply in that HTF used.

The reason you wanted us to put the puzzle pieces together instead of
handing over the golden keys is for us to accumulate the chart
experiences (screen hours), and figure out what HTF and LTF would work,
