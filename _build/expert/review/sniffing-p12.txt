### sniffing_p12_19
EN: Got a PM that better be answered in public...

The message was saying that in our early days of traditional S/D trading we used to look for candle engulfing pattern and on RTM here almost nobody uses it but the engulf of the zone is in play. So, I have a question: why don't you use the engulfing candle pattern and why do you treat the zone engulfing as more reliable?

My answer is:

I still look at candle engulf when I look for S/D clusters and I look at other candle patterns as well, but the engulfing of areas is more reliable because "Price Dynamics" is not just a simple candle pattern but "a study of price in motion in regards to the supply & demand order flow", in other words; "the history of price action/reaction and behavior in relation to the supply & demand".

Moreover, the candle engulf on HTF could be an engulf of a significant area on LTF, the areas could be decision points or highs and lows which are areas of significant interest and engulfing them means price wants to travel beyond that area, as well as bears or bulls on the other side are starting to weaken.

What if you’re looking at the line chart and you don’t have any candles?!

Think of candle engulfing as the basics of S/D, and the area engulfing as the advanced S/D.
