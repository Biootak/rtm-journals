 be witnessed in Price Approach Reaction. For me I look for a Big
picture, where price is going and come from. I find the LFL/UFL kinks I

for confluence with a trick, PCP or SR kinks. LS being the most strong

 then decide where its come from and whether we have PA, or can I TT
based on the other factors and the approach. IE are retailers getting in
 early and being trapped.
