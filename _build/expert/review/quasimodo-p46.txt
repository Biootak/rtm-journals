### quasimodo_p46_1131
EN: Hello everyone. This is the first time I post some charts. I would like to thank IF and all other for the fantastic education available here. Three months ago I was still plotting MAs and macds

. Yesterday I was trying to make sense from my boxes, zones etc

when suddenly I realised that I had a beautiful QM happening just in front of my nose. It was the first time I saw one live. It's terrifying. I initially took screenshots of the differents stages of the move but It was too messy so I will put only the first one and then the whole move in cleaner charts. What I took from this: For a QM to be valid you want: -an EG of a FL/ftr from a pole into consolidation. -a FO of the consolidation ( which is in fact the filling of the HTF SD) -a second engulf of your FL back into the pole as confirmation. -Then you wait at QM level. -If you miss it or are not filled you have a second chance because the price will pull back at the level of the initial FL. I understand the reference to the hunchback Quasimodo from the book but personally would have named it Hannibal the Cannibal... Or Jack the Ripper

I am not sure of all this so help and comments are welcome! Have a great week end.
