### rinehart_p9_22
EN: Considering Frosty's recent journal posts as well as some PMs from
newer members asking for advice about learning RTM and trading in
general, I'd like to give a little perspective:

Learning the materials taught at RTM does not mean you have to trade the way IF trades. In trading the only thing that truly counts is having a positive equity curve! IF is able to nail near zero drawdown, high R trades. That is something to strive for but it is not the main goal.

I think some members get so caught up trying to learn how to nail
perfect RTM trades, they lose sight of the most important goal. Your
purpose with RTM has to be to find a way to read the market better.
Armed with a better understanding of where price wants to go and where
it will not violate, you can find ways to trade that match your
personality and situation.

AK is a 1 min scalper. I trade larger time frames mostly set and forget
 due to work. This works fine for both of us. On the other hand, I am
not a scalper and AK isn't comfortable with set and forget. The fact is
 both of us have incorporated RTM into what works for us and we use it
to grow our equity. Could AK learn to set and forget or could I learn
to be a scalper? Possibly, but we don't have to. We have found
something that works for us. We can stay in our lane, rinse and repeat.
 With RTM, you don't need to learn how to nail a 5 pip SL 150 R/R
trade. You achieve your goals by growing your equity curve.

Case in point, here is a trade I took on Gold today. My entry was
absolutely not a RTM entry. This is not a trade I would normally post
on RTM because it doesn't fit with the way members are taught to trade.
 We are taught to sell at supply yet this was a breakout trade. I took
the trade because I knew where price wanted to go. RTM gave me
knowledge of the engulf and I used that knowledge to trade what was
given.

FOCUS ON GROWING YOUR EQUITY, PERIOD. Take from RTM what you need to become profitable, period.
