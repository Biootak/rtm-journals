Hi Samjardavid-

A FL is any FTR that holds price up or down whereas a ORFL is an origin up/down and a FL.

ORFL is often the target of longs/shorts, so they hold special
importance. Salivan discusses the concept of magnetic attraction vs.
repulsion and this is related specifically to ORFL's.
