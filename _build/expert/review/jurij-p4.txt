### jurij_p4_11
EN: There is only one playing field, the one made up by ITs.

What I mean, there's no range/trend or other "choppy" market conditions, only the right scale.

When the market trends (nothing to the left) then ITs are driving price
on the HTF playing field, likeweise range/chop creates the LTF field.
