EN: I'm making transition from FT2 to real- time charts (demo). Very
important thing I need to learn is not to miss trades that I am able to
spot and execute flawlessly.

When I miss perfect entry, and this happens quite often, I have urge to
find another great setup as soon as possible. I don't look at situation
objectivly, and spotted setup is not even nearly as good as the missed
one. Solution to this problem is this:

- be aware of this behaviour/emotion and get it under control

- don't miss trades

Everyday there is a trade with good RR (at least 5R) that I've seen
many times already. It is just matter of letting go all mediocre setups
 and being ready to execute one or two trades a day when the best
opportunity occurs.

How to do that?

I am in the process of putting into practice what I mentioned in my post #219.

I reduced number of instruments I watch. Now it is 7 and in next week I
 will decide whether I schould increase or decrease this number.

Every day after NY session I will review all trade opportunities (mostly
 LTF scalps) that occured that day. I need to be aware that I can let go
 even a few setups in a row, because soon there will be another great
opportunity and it is worth to PATIENTLY wait for it.

Every instrument I watch is analysed from Monthly to M1 timeframe, and I
 often go lower then that (ticks). Scalp opportunities may pop up very
quickly on the market

so I set alerts at important levels, and I update my analysis many times a day.
