Here I will post my homework about compression.

In this first chart, I saw a lot of points of compression, but it seems that the only properly called CP is the fourth one. The three first are flags. The fifth one is a triangle or a pennant.

CP is a reversal pattern, while flags are continuation patterns. But it seems they share their nature, that is the consumption of orders while at the same time price is taked to the desire level, cleaning the way through for the next movement.

In this chart, why price has to pole to the level, instead of compressed all the way up? In the CP example (the main one, in the center of the chart) we could see how price is taken down, breaking the most recent lows, creating a pinbar that lets the shorts trapped, giving them fuel till the Supply target level. The second chart I attach explains my view on this in detail, based on the awesome information that is being discussed in the Tyoon thread
