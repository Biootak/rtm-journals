I've marked this price drop day as Day 1. To my eyes, the around area around here is a RBD (in the lower timeframes you should also see a DBD) short-term supply level with the nearest area of D1 demand located around the lower wide red line. With the close above and local supply and demand levels in close proximity we must at least now consider the idea that big money has started the process of accumulation.

The day after the drop, i.e. "second day", price pins the area I've marked with a short red line. What caused this pin to occur? The only thing that can cause this pinning above to occur is aggressive selling further confirming our assessment that supply exceeded demand at this "level".

You'll see on the candle (again I've not marked it in order to try and keep the chart clean) immediately to the right of "second day" pin bar that price gapped up into the level and then sold off again back down to the close of the second day pinbar. What causes this to happen and why? Once again it is selling. Why is selling occuring here? In order for big money to move the market up it has to buy. Who does it buy from? Sellers. Where do sellers and selling occur? At supply levels. What do supply levels look like? You know the answer to this question because you've already marked loads of them on your charts:)
