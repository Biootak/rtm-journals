EN>>>EUR-AUD - Continued from post above:

Taking a closer look at this RBD to ID more important swap zones....

2 engulfs 1

B engulfs A

D engulfs C

So which of these boxes represents the origin of the move down? Sometimes it is hard to know, but it is really important because origins are also future targets. Not knowing the future target means you don't know an ORFL when you see one. Not knowing a target/ORFL = not knowing if an engulf is valid and how far price may RT before hitting the target.

It can be hard to ID an origin, because often times there are multiple origins within different time scales.

What process do we use to identify an origin? Generally, they occur after an engulf of an important swap zone...but there has to more to it...especially when there are multiple engulfs of swap zones all located in close proximity together (as in this EUR-AUD example. It is easy to look back in hindsight and label origins and targets, because history gives us a complete view of price reaction. The difficulty is in identifying these origins/targets in real time. I would refine that statement by adding: the difficulty is in identifying the relevant origins/targets in real time, within the proper scale of the engulf you are using to begin your trade.

Every engulf has a target. Valid (actionable) engulfs have unmet targets

If every engulf has a target, we can safely assume there are many engulfs and many targets all existing in relatively close proximity to each other. How do we go about keeping it all straight?

Here are some of my ideas on this - I have been spending a lot of time on this question because I am convinced this is the key to success in trading.

Firstly: as intimidating as the issue of scale is, I think it is actually a major benefit to us traders...if we can learn how to use scale to improve and normalize our analysis. I am still very new to this, so I am also learning how to "use" scale to my benefit.

Scale offers us a chance to focus on one engulf/target at a time. LTF engulfs and targets will get satisfied first (by first, I mean the lower scale time frame engulfs/targets get met before HTF targets) This happens because PA is fractal, and in order for price to move long distances to satisfy a HTF target, it must pass through countless smaller zones full of LTF engulfs and targets. This is also where the PAZ concept comes in.

As confusing as the PAZ is, I think its intention is actually to simplify trading.

A PAZ is defined as the space between two FTR's/FL's. Regardless of the scale, a PAZ exists between two very important decision points (the areas of true supply and true demand exist within the FTR's that contain the PAZ. In order to break out of a PAZ, price must break through one of these important FTR's. Until this happens, price will bounce around within the PAZ.

How does price move out of a PAZ? I think it all goes back to FOF. Strong orders must exist to break previously important levels within a FTR. Strong orders means the bulls and bear fight it out, the team with the most liquidity wins.

Breaking a strong demand zone requires ample sell orders, and breaking a strong supply zone requires ample buy orders.

مقیاس به ما فرصتی می‌دهد که هر بار روی یک اینگافل/تارگت تمرکز کنیم. اینگافل‌ها و تارگت‌های تایم‌فریم پایین‌تر اول برآورده می‌شوند (منظورم از اول این است که اینگافل‌ها/تارگت‌های تایم‌فریم مقیاس پایین‌تر قبل از تارگت‌های تایم‌فریم بالا برآورده می‌شوند) این اتفاق می‌افتد چون PA فراکتالی است و برای اینکه قیمت مسافت‌های بلند را برای برآورده کردن تارگت تایم‌فریم بالا طی کند، باید از بی‌شمار ناحیهٔ کوچک‌تر پر از اینگافل‌ها و تارگت‌های تایم‌فریم پایین‌تر عبور کند. اینجاست که مفهوم PAZ هم وارد می‌شود.

هرچقدر PAZ گیج‌کننده به نظر برسد، فکر می‌کنم قصدش در واقع ساده کردن ترید است.

PAZ به‌عنوان فضای بین دو FTR/FL تعریف می‌شود. بدون توجه به مقیاس، PAZ فقط وقتی PAZ است که با دو FTR کنترل‌کننده احاطه شده باشد (یکی کنترل‌شدهٔ نزولی است، دیگری کنترل‌شدهٔ صعودی). PAZ محصورشده بین این دو FTR اساساً میدان نبرد PA است که هدفش به دست آوردن FOF کافی برای شکست یکی از FTRهای مخالف است.

مفهوم PAZ روی چارت تیک درست است و روی چارت ماهانه هم درست است. تنها تفاوت بین PAZ تایم‌فریم بالا و PAZ تایم‌فریم پایین‌تر این است که PAZهای بعدی زیادی داخل PAZ تایم‌فریم بالا وجود دارد. PAZها همه به یک دلیل شکل می‌گیرند و همه به یک دلیل می‌شکنند: order flow.

Let's keep analyzing this concept of targets/origins and scale...

Since the issue of scale adds complexity, I will begin by listing all components of PA that occur regardless of scale. Instead of 'laws of physics' it is my version of 'laws of price movement'

Please don't hesitate to tell me if I am wrong about any of this... I

am still very new to this

We know the following is always true, regardless of scale:

Each PAZ is bordered by a FTR/True supply/demand/associated MPL: Regardless of scale, a PAZ is only a PAZ when bordered by two controlling FTR's (one is bear controlled, the other is bull controlled). The PAZ contained between these two FTR's is essentially a PA battleground aimed at obtaining enough FOF to break one of the opposing FTR's.

The concept of a PAZ is true in a tick chart, and it is true on a monthly chart.The only difference between a HTF PAZ and a LTF PAZ is there are a lot more subsequent PAZ's within the HTF PAZ. The PAZ's all form for the same reason, and they all break for the same reason: order flow.

Analogy: the laws of gravity effect you whether you fall off a skyscraper or fall off a ladder...the only difference is how far you fall from the time you lose balance until the time you hit the ground. You pass through a lot more space on your way down from the roof of the skyscraper. There are more objects you must pass through before hitting the ground (trees, awnings, shrubs, birds, people walking along the sidewalk below, etc....)

But.. the inevitable consequence of gravity is you fall & hit the ground regardless.

I think the same can be said for price movement inside and throughout PAZ's.

In fact, a HTF chart will all of the origins and targets of various scaled (marked out with rectangles) sort of resembles a high rise building with each rectangle representing a floor of the building.

You can't fall from a high rise without passing through every floor on your way down. You can't hit a HTF target until you first pass through all the other LTF PAZ's/origins/target in your way.
