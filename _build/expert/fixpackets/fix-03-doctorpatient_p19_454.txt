Mel, all trades are taken with at least the 4hr chart in mind where possible. I want to have the minimum time frame with me when London opens. There are times when a setup comes after the first 4r period has elapsed after the London open. A pull back into the origin is common for my entries, at times I will take them early and scale in as the setup gets more orders in the market. Once I like what I see there is no hesitation and enter on the next closed candle. Pro money rarely gives a second shot an entry. If you have doubts, dont take it.

My check list is all mental these days, but in the early days I did have a checklist taped to my monitors. My journal is my trading posted into excel. Anyone starting off is wise to jot down all trades and any losers need careful analysis to see why it failed. There are trades which will fail even with the perfect setup, there is no such thing as a sure thing in trading.

Always maintain the longer time frames in your mind and know where each one is in the cycle.

This is the basis of being properly prepared.

Les_p, pro money wont do the same thing twice. They will do all possible to hide their movements and only show their true intentions after false moves and pain for the retail trader. This is the single most difficult part to figure out in trading. The changes only have a few set 'shapes' people look for, such as long wicks and tails. Some trade what they call pin bars only and design trading systems around them, but this is only a tiny part of the puzzle. The only way to see what pro money is doing is to spend a lot of time looking at charts. Good formations from candles form around support and resistance areas, which turn into supply and demand.

Castus, I dont place much emphasis on correlations because these will change over time and you cannot put much importance into them. At the moment look at the S&P500 and the AUD, over the past few months both are diverging. In my view too many traders look at too many other factors which are meant to have influence on a certain pair. I trade what I see on the chart of the pair I am interested, no external factors influence me unless price is at an important area and major news is due. Trading at a time like that will result in the price movement we saw last Friday in eur/usd which messed up a nice long setup and drove price down on nothing but emotion and the overwhelming new supply will have given pro money a lot of pain. There are times when the pro's cant hold an area.

Les_p، پول حرفه‌ای دو بار یک کار را تکرار نمی‌کند. آن‌ها هر کاری می‌کنند تا حرکاتشان را پنهان کنند و قصد واقعی‌شان را فقط بعد از حرکت‌های فیک و رنج‌دادن معامله‌گران خرده‌فروش نشان می‌دهند. این سخت‌ترین بخش تشخیص در معامله است. تغییرات فقط چند «شکل» مشخص دارند که آدم‌ها دنبالشان می‌گردند، مثل سایه‌های بلند و دم‌ها. بعضی‌ها فقط همان را معامله می‌کنند که «پین‌بار» می‌خوانند و سیستم معاملاتی دور آن‌ها طراحی می‌کنند، اما این فقط بخش کوچکی از پازل است. تنها راه دیدن کار پول حرفه‌ای، وقت گذاشتن زیاد روی چارت‌هاست. فرم‌های خوب از کندل‌ها حوالی نواحی حمایت و مقاومت شکل می‌گیرند که به عرضه و تقاضا تبدیل می‌شوند.
