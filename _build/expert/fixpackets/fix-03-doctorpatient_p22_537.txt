Lets really get into the details now.

Here is a zoomed in view of the last leg down in price. To help guide your eye here, candle 1 was a race to grab old demand and is also taken at support.

2 the bulls have already got their work done and the follow through buy intraday bears is weak the close well off the lows.

3 the bulls intent is obvious here with a very large body and close with very little wick on top. On the other chart you will see this candle hit old supply.

4 considering we just hit old supply this candle close showed such weak bullish momentum that it was plain to see we need to fall back down, or collect more orders to break above.

5 a weak bearish candle, note how it closed well inside the previous candles low, note the long tail, a sign of buying.

6 a very strong bull candle with strong close above support, so we long this puppy to the moon now right?

7 no, we wait for a pull back and see does support hold. And 7 is a perfect bearish engulfing candle and proves candle 6 was a false bullish break.

8 unfortunately the market timing for the previous move was not very good and we are now stuck between close in old supply and demand. When this happens we need to keep the big picture in mind, we are still below supply. This candle attempts to grab more orders from the bulls by the tail showing buying, however the weak close tells is its going nowhere.

9 we have a break below near term candle 5 and old demand from candle 3. But hitting candle 3's demand hard means we have to see a reaction higher.

10 this is the reaction from candle 9, it has setup up pro money for the main bearish move.

11 this is interesting, the candle pushed lower than the previous candles wick and closed within the body of candle 3 which is still the most significant candle we need to break.

12 finally a strong break and strong close. Of note here is the candle got into the tail of candle 1. There were a lot of buyers here and even though it is not the first time price has been in that area, there is validity given to this area given its likely hood to act as resistance once broken and retested.

13 no interest in taking price lower yet and no progress made by bulls or bears. The very slight tail below the tiny body means we wait.

14 this is the start of the pro bears selling. See how the wick pulls in supply from candle 12 and closes as a bullish looking candle but with a very low close on the candle 12 body. Books will tell you this is a bullish engulfing candle because it had contained the previous candle, correct, but, the books fail to tell you the reason for the wick and where it went.

15 Something to look at on this candle is the fact that the body is both bearish and closed within the previous candle, but no takers higher considering candle 14 is what is considered bullish. Pro money know better.

16 This is the bug push to get the market running and all the work prior to this candle was done in candle 14 and a lot of bears waited for this move whereas candle 14 would have got day traders long and now candle 16 would have stopped them out adding fuel to the bearish orders. Also note candles 12, 13, 14, 15 are all at S/R.

17 not much to say, we are moving down at a nice clip and money being made by the bears, dont stand in front of this freight train, be on it.

18 The line at the bottom of my chart is old demand taken from the chart earlier up. So why has price suddenly stopped and gone bullish?....two reasons, not all pro money has the same take profit target and some will bail before the ultimate target. This candle shows profit taking, which in itself has pinned the last of the bears who entered at the close of 16 which is 17's open. Who would have guessed it setup one final small move down. But on this time frame is enough to buy a nice lunch:)

19 old demand pinned to the pixel and also the first sign of the market slowing down. The tail tells us this. When the market is running hard it will not stop on a dime and the tails and wicks you see at major old price level on higher time frames has to be traded against in the opposite direction by pro money to first stop the current move and setup the next. This is never a cheap exercise and costs a lot of money. We see the wicks and tails and look for our entries.

20. Given pro money reached their target it was of no surprise we will at least see one small leg up and possibly a retest of the newly created low.

21 so far we are no longer bearish and now bullish, but a retest of the low is still on the cards unless we see a test of new support coming along and this may look like a decision point. Possible places for a sensible decision are candle 18, 13 thru 15 cluster.

The time frame for this doesnt matter because price which is plotted in time is a fractal of itself and keeping all the old support and demand areas from higher time frames for use on lower time frame charts is essential for success. An example would be a 1 or 5 min trader taking a short at candle 19 because it was a break of 18, but not understanding that he was shorting into a major higher time frame of buyers.

The other horizontal lines on the chart are a partial guide to help train your eye and logical side of your brain. There are more places to find support and resistance and I will let you look for those.

If you are willing to wait price will always show you a clear direction and when its turned. There are times when even a 5min trade wont come around for a day or so.
