Hi gxp1,

Your homework looks good though I would encourage you not to use the zigzag indicator for the following reasons.

1. It will train your eyes faster and better not using the indicator. Instead, focus on the raw price. It may initially be a little more challenging but you will gain much more from your homework exercises.

2. You will miss the more important and subtle RBDs and DBRs on your chart as the indicator will go straight past them. Some of the more important RBDs and DBRs are small subtle ones as opposed to the obvious ones that catches everyone’s eye.

This post by IF is in this thread is a gem! (I have placed IF's image below.)

The small DBR IF points out towards the bottom of the chart (blue box – the ‘giant omission’) would not be picked up via the indicator and is an important DBR. It’s also an example of how price reading requires us to piece all the individual parts of price action together.

Why is that small DBR important?

The DBR shows where true demand is within the FTR which forms after an engulf of an important barrier. In case it’s not obvious, The following diagram explains the idea.
