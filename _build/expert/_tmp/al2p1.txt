The question is: Should we not bear in mind the orange FL as well as this forms over the break of RS also?

I would say that the answer is yes (agreeing with Edge).

However, in the video the issue IF was talking about is "how S/R trader-retailers see S/R or S/R flip" and taught us we should not look to trade at S/R point/level only, but instead look at the FL that is in line with that S/R. RTMers will see price bounce above S/R or below S/R and still see it reacting to S/R flip as long as price is reacting correctly to its FL. In contrast, retailers are so fanatic about price having to react to the exact S/R point to the pip, so if price has not reached S/R level they think price still has not reached its target, or if price pierced beyond S/R level they think price breaks the level out and will go to the next S/R.

If the question is, where are the other FLs, then yes the orange FL need to be considered too, as well as lots of FLs in lower scale as seen in LTF.
