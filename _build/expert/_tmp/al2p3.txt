Hi EM, A FL occur occurs anytime a barrier is broken, retraces, and advances to hit an opposing TG. Ergo, it also Fails To Return. When price comes back in the opposite direction and takes out the FL, its once the MPL of the opposing FL is taken out that the new FL becomes a UFL or LFL (once it also hits its TG). Its Ping Pong back and forth.

RBDs DBRs Caps etc are exercises for your eyes to see the swings. These swings cannot be relied on to turn price. Nevertheless they are important because we can look at a swing and ask:

Should Price have turned here? Which of course we can answer because FO's can be predicted.

The only time I use a cap RBD/DBR as an actual trade is if its the MPL/LS trick. Which just looks like a little hiccup (FL/kink) on a higher scale but looks like an extreme MPL on the entry scale
--- FA ---
