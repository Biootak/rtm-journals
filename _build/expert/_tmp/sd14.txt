We look for decisions on value, and decision points both respected and broken.

If a demand gets broken, and price shoots down from it, we look for the point where the decision was made to break it. Ideally we look for a break, and then a rally back into it which fails miserably. This "Epic Fail" point is the decision that price has broken the level and the level is staying broken. We can't just expect price to break this DP easily, as lots of sell orders await price's return there.

That's why price will often need to bounce from S/R flips back to the last DP before advancing.

In the case of your chart, the Deman you have shown is a reaction to something on the left - the decision to break a beautiful Supply. See what happened lately when price came back up from your buy line and hit that old supply?
