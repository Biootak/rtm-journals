Alientrader,

Thank you for your question.

I have limited time and there is no set time slot when i can say, ok, this week i can spend the same 3 hours at my screen every day. And to be really honest i dont want to sit at my screen for hours.

My trading style works round my lifestyle, not the other way round. You see i have traded full time, but i found it boring and i felt i needed to be in the market all the time to make money. These days i dont chase price around, i let the market come to me!

So considering that i can not always be there to pull the trigger or even watch the approach. I set buy / sell limit orders on really good zones that are fresh, with a view on HTF. This affords me time and you generally get good momentum away from the zone, i can then switch to trade management mode.

I set zones on my laptop or desktop, and have alarms by my entries, these alarms send an email and text to my mobile phone.I have an app for my platform on my phone. So, if i am busy at least i know a trade has been triggered.

When i get an hour or so to watch, i scan through pairs, suss the general trend and sometimes i get in on a uptrending market on a pull back to demand. Like catching a move part way through, as im sure you know, price can give us multiple opportunities to get into a move.

The 2 charts above, are both good zones. But, one thing i am looking at more is, where is price going to turn and how can i see that before hand. Most of the time i know that price will react to a zone, but how can i be accurate with my limit orders.

1 thing that frustrates me is when entering a zone, does price react on the spikes or bodies of candles. To me they both seem to happen. So here is my latest logic.

Lets say my account is £10,000

My total risk on any one trade is 1%, that = £100, for me to risk in a zone

so in a marked box zone of 50 pips top to bottom

This allows me a lot size of £2 per pip. So if my trade fails i have only lost £100 or 1% of my account.

Now, if i have 1 limit order with the above equation, it allows me one chance to enter the market.

But if i break the lot size down to 0.50p per pip, i can have 4 limit orders at various areas in the zone. And still only risk 1%!

So when price enters my zone it could pick up some or all orders.

This allows me with metatrader to also take part profits on 1 or 2 of those micro orders, as price hits mid targets. If i have one order on IBFX, i can not part close 1 large order.

Sorry the explanation was long winded,hopefully you can see my full reasoning behind a method that suits my trading and my lifestyle.

Regards
