@@@ supplydemand_p15_364 | Tyoon
rokuhaato wrote: If price form a flag before reaching S or D area, what does it mean and how to deal with it?

I am sorry if I am in the wrong thread to ask.

When you see something that looks like a flag before a level, it is most likely consumption of that level. So if you see a buflag at supply it could be that the buyers are consuming the supply orders to break through.

If you think of a level of supply and a level of demand (or support and resistance), price either consumes one level and rejects off of the other. So price can be consuming supply then rejecting off of demand. So, the way to deal with it is to find your levels and watch the PA interaction between them within the flag keeping the bigger picture in mind.
