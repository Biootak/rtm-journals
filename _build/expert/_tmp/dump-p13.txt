So I started thinking about FTR PAZ's. Price moves around in consolidation limited by the FTR and a cap both of which are often tradeable on FTB. Then it either breaks the consolidation by breaking the FTR in the direction it came from or by breaking the cap and continuing on - either way price creates a new FTR in a new PAZ, and when drawn on a chart, it looks like the boxing you've drawn in post 321. Sometimes it can be a bit difficult to find the exact extents of the box or when several overlap, and I imagine this is a bit harder still when trying "to box" the right edge in real-time. But step-by-step we'll get there.

A traditional supply/demand trader could probably also argue that you've just boxed a bunch of bases of RBR, DBD, DBR, and RBD's.
