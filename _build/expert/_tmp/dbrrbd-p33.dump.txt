When price actually arrives at the level, you can see that price is able to penetrate the zone quite deeply, Which indicates that the level isn't as strong as I thought it would be. We do get an rejection though (long upper wick on the rejection candle) What I've read about stacked orders/zones is that in a supply zone, the higher in the zone the more orders there are. Which would be explainable by looking at this example. Price was able to penetrate the zone, but got rejected in the top of the zone, so there were a lot of sell orders stacked there) Price then spends some time trading somewhat sideways, and then enters the zone again, followed by a big drop, to the demand zone (that I didn't mark) and from there visits the zone for a third and even fourth time, The forth time it penetrated the zone deeply again consuming all the orders that were left and broke the zone.
