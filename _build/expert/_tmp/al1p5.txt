First, we must dissect the kink, and mark all the clean orders in it. Then we watch the approach for distinct stop hunts into these levels. We can trade each and any of them, taking profits to cover trips to the next sets of clean orders.

Say our first minor set of clean orders breaks. Price will, sooner or later get to the next one. It could first retrace for ten pips, or ten thousand. But it will get there. So, essentially, our minor level hasn't turned price.

Can you think of a way to manage a trade taken at a kink which broke, allowing for no loss and the possibility of a giant retrace?
