All of the trades I take are either FTR setups or reverse FTRs. The latter is nothing but an ignored higher low or lower high. The trick here is to give the trades enough room to breath while it may go against you a bit, and more importantly to close out early. A lot of times my RR is not really a good one per RR rules. That's because I will mostly close at the first area of opposition. I have deleted from my system the notion of "Price will/should break" because we are coming from HTF SD or what have you. In the event it does break I will re-enter. It is indeed tiresome to be watching and micromanaging entries and exist. But the advantage is really no more than a couple of hours work per day.
