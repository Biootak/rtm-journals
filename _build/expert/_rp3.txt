Take my trades from the edge of zones. If a zones is engulfed, look for an entry at the origin on the other side of the zone price has engulfed from. Targets should be the origin of price at the edge of a zone. If the origin and edge get engulfed, hold the trade and target the origin at the edge of the zone that has be engulfed.
