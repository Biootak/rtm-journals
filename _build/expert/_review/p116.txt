Hello HCDE, first time poster here, so please I am not trying to sound patronizing at all,
 A 'range' is typically the distance from swing high to swing low, so
when we talk about range we need to be specific about what timeframes.
In the picture IF posted, when he talked about dealers (or IT) that
needs to fill a range, he most likely refer to a HTF range as depicted
in my drawing below. So imagine on a HTF scale, price has already
established a range from A to B. So when IT bring the price back to C,
the movement from B to C is called 'filling the range'. Likewise,
movement of C to D, and then from D back to E, are all called filling
the range.

Best regards

Astro
