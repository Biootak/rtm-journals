EN: Morning everybody,

I think I just had some sort of aha-moment.

For that reason, I wanted to post it here, so that I have had it written
 down, which makes me remember it more easiliy, and I can find it back.
That important of a memory is it, for me at least. For others, this may
be perfectly clear.

Little background information to start with:

Price moves in poles and flags. Poles exist either of simple poles, or
compression. Below, on top of, or somewhere in the middle of a pole, a
flag/cap forms. Flags are created by kinks, or Flag Limits, which in
their turn, are created by barriers such as PCP, LS, RS.

Now for the epiphany: Flags exist between 2 Flag Limits.

Easy as that. FL's have edges, and a flag sits right between FL1 for
example and FL2. This is stated in the article, I know, but somehow it
didn't make me really understand that. It is however, of major
importance in reading price.

I had a lot of trouble understanding the concept of Flag Limits,
although it didn't have anything to do with finding the darn things. I
do get when something is a FL but what I didn't seem to grasp, was when
they were of any use and how they would act in a chart. Sure, I've seen
price reacting the FL's but still. For some, this might seem really
silly, for me, it wasn't clear at all. Taking 3 days off off charting,
reading, commenting did it's job I guess, so there's a big lesson as
well. Learn stuff, go over it until you get it, and most importantly, let your mind process everything. It'll put together what needs to be put together and that's something you can't force.

Now, If someone doesn't agree with what's stated above, please feel free
 to elaborate on that. I'm still just a newbie and might be wrong about
things. I'm really eager to learn, so all feedback is very welcome.
Thank's IF, you've stated quite a few times that it's really important
to let stuff sink in, but experiencing it sometimes is the only way to
let that sink in. And thanks RTM, for being such an inspirational place!

من در فهم مفهوم FL‌ها خیلی مشکل داشتم، هرچند هیچ ربطی به پیدا کردن این چیزهای لعنتی نداشت. می‌فهم چه زمانی چیزی FL است، اما چیزی که ظاهراً درک نمی‌کردم این بود که کِی به درد می‌خورند و در چارت چطور رفتار می‌کنند. مطمئناً رفتار قیمت نسبت به FL‌ها را دیده‌ام، اما باز هم... برای بعضی‌ها این ممکن است واقعاً احمقانه به نظر برسد، برای من اصلاً روشن نبود. 3 روز فاصله گرفتن از چارت‌نویسی، خواندن و کامنت گذاشتن کار خودش را کرد، پس درس بزرگی هم دارد. چیزها را یاد بگیر، رویشان بمان تا بگیریشان، و مهم‌تر از همه، بگذار ذهنت همه‌چیز را پردازش کند. ذهن آنچه را باید کنار هم بگذارد کنار هم می‌گذارد و این کاری نیست که بتوانی به زور انجامش دهی.
