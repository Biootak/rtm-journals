EN: The next post is sort of a addition to what was discussed recently, found in Salivan's journal.

In my opinion, it's a good way to read price on the way up, since it tells you lots!

Here goes:

1: Price broke through barrier, wants next.

2: Broke straight through, whats next.

3: Broken,

4: Broken immediately after, same with 5.

6: price touches it and retraces directly. This way, you'll know that
price is going down until it hits the next target, which you can find
the same way.

Not the quickest way to see live where price is bouncing but a fun exercise!

Now it's time to find out what makes that one a stronger level of
resistance, so that it's possible to anticipate on those levels.
