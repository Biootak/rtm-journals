EN: What really stands out to me after dissecting this trade is the concept of price playing 'tricks' in the past. 'Tricks' is really just another way to say fakeout, right?

And we don't want just any fakeout...they happen all the time. We want FO's that occur to the MPL, with a resulting engulf to the opposite side so we know which way price is likely to go (in the direction of the engulf) and we know where price is not likely to FO (back to the MPL, once a FO has already occurred here).

These tricks help us to "zero in" on the important zone of FOF that is likely to react when price returns. The tricks are like road maps that tell us where price has already gone...

Isn't the ultimate goal to find the last available area of FOF within the scope and scale of our trade? Knowing where that last pocket of liquidity exists tells us where price is likely to go before turning..... If we know where price will be attracted (like a magnet to the last pocket of FOF that hasn't been consumed) we know exactly where to enter the market with the least amount of DD.
