=== if_p109_20 hash=4ab45efd8ae56cf291ed99c12b50b0136b3fdd8f author=Ifmyante
EN: Right. To answer a PM I got, which was

"Is there any way to know how far will the retrace will happen after the break of the level?"

No.It can retrace to any set of clean orders in the zone it's traveled through.

Ideally it will travel all the way back for a QM at the MPL, or even
break the H/L for a DM, but it often only retraces a short way, leaving
the clean orders beyond for later.

At each minor level it retraces to, we can take confirmed LTF trades,
taking some profits to cover losses, letting some run to the HTF target,
 should this be the depth of the RT
