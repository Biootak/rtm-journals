there are two price,one is ASK price another is BID price

when you BUY (open a long position) you always buy at the ASK Pr ice

when you SELL (open a short position) you always sell at the BID price.

The ASK price is always above the current price(BID price) you see on your chart.

The price you see on your chart is the BID price

[/b]

BUY orders are executed at ASK price, but MT4 charts do not show ASK price by

default. This is why on the chart it looks like the trade was filled too early without price touching the entry level. But ASK price touched the entry price, it’s just not displayed on

the chart anywhere.

Sell orders are executed at BID Prices. That’s why you will see price (BID Price) hit the

entry price level and get activated immediately.

so..what should we do to avoid some loss?remember ITs are always trying to use your order for their benifit..

so we can see in scenario 1 that price crossed our entry line thus trade triggered and hit our SL.but we know that we buy at ASK price and here the ASK price is still with in our FL, but as the SL of buy order will trigger when BID price touche that..thus here we are out from the trade with a loss..to avoid this..we should place buy order SL below our (FL low-spread)...in scenario 2..we see that price crossed our entry level but trade not activated..again the reason is ASK price which is above BID price and here it not touched our entry level thus trade not triggured yet..and from here price can go in the direction of the trade..but you could not enter the trade here and you will get frustrated and will take bad decisions..what actually IT wants..so to avoid this..we should place our pending buy order above (level high line+spread)

in scenario 3..we see a sell limit order..now here how can IT tricks you? we can see the price(BID) is still with in the level but the ASK price crossed your SL and we know that in case of sell..BID price triggers entry and ASK price triggers SL..thus we get entered and get stop out much before our SL in the chart.as we only see BID price..to avoid this..we should place our stop above (upper level+spread).I know that this is a very basic thing,but felt trouble for this so am posting here..THNX everyone for your direct or indirect support
