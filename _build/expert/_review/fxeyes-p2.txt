--- fxeyes_p2_3 author=Ifmyante hash=6b4924d8
EN: In words:

Draw the line from the LS.

Go to the right hand side of the swing high/low.

Find where price, coming from the H/L, broke the line.

Draw LSK from the deepest retrace after the break, back to the furthest advance before the retrace.

Should there be smaller kinks in LTF on the right edge of this kink,
mark them too, as clean orders here are what allow us to get to the pip
entries, right at the LS price.

Lots of words, I know, but try to apply them literally to a couple of
charts, noting all the steps, and you will never forget the technique

 explained in the 2nd RTM Lite video, and other s/r lines that look
important:

1. The top and bottom of FLs that broke something significant, and PCP, CM break (as explained in the video). Monthly to H4.

2. A price area where there are multiple touches (the best seems to be
the one that reacted from both the top and bottom). The more touches
the better.

3. In the more immediate term, if i'm expecting price to move a certain
way (for example, price poled down, and then retracing up), i'd look
left for any levels that looked 'interesting' for a potential s/r flip.

learning. Sometimes its just a 'feeling' based on past experience. It
can get really messy, and I don't use all the lines. But if a line
happen to line up with an LS, FL, CM, or some other price structure,
i'll zoom out to see if the line is likely to be relevant.

EN: Hi there,

I would like to give a small suggestion :)

When we draw left shoulder kinks, it is always better to draw the whole
kink in that scale, not just a part of it. Then you can clearly see
which point first reacted to the kink and then decide if it is a QM or a
 DM. The trick you marked actually reacted to the original LS kink if
you carefully see. Then engulfed into compression. Then retraced back up
 and came down.
