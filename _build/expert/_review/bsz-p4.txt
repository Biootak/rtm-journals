EN: Yes quite True Linos. The only thing I would say is that because we do not need PA, the trade off is that we ought to really be looking at the approach and the clean orders we are leaning on. for example, lets use a 2DT example. even without space we can take the trade back to the 2DT kink

BUT

We need to be comfortable with the approach and reaction at our FL we are leaning on, plus they need to contain clean orders. So the trade off here is not having to worry so much about the space (as per diagram - QM has hit target) but we have to be more cautious than we ordinarily would with the approach and reaction.
