For that reason, I wanted to post it here, so that I have had it written down, which makes me remember it more easiliy, and I can find it back. That important of a memory is it, for me at least. For others, this may be perfectly clear. Little background information to start with: Price moves in poles and flags. Poles exist either of simple poles, or compression. Below, on top of, or somewhere in the middle of a pole, a flag/cap forms. Flags are created by kinks, or Flag Limits, which in their turn, are created by barriers such as PCP, LS, RS. Now for the epiphany: Flags exist between 2 Flag Limits . Easy as that. FL's have edges, and a flag sits right between FL1 for example and FL2. This is stated in the article, I know, but somehow it didn't make me really understand that. It is however, of major importance in reading price. I had a lot of trouble understanding the concept of Flag Limits, although it didn't have anything to do with finding the darn things. I do get when something is a FL but what I didn't seem to grasp, was when they were of any use and how they would act in a chart. Sure, I've seen price reacting the FL's but still. For some, this might seem really silly, for me, it wasn't clear at all. Taking 3 days off off charting, reading, commenting did it's job I guess, so there's a big lesson as well. Learn stuff, go over it until you get it, and most importantly, let your mind process everything. It'll put together what needs to be put together and that's something you can't force. Now, If someone doesn't agree with what's stated above, please feel free to elaborate on that. I'm still just a newbie and might be wrong about things. I'm really eager to learn, so all feedback is very welcome. For others, this may be perfectly clear. Little background information to start with: Price moves in poles and flags. Poles exist either of simple poles, or compression. Below, on top of, or somewhere in the middle of a pole, a flag/cap forms. Flags are created by kinks, or Flag Limits, which in their turn, are created by barriers such as PCP, LS, RS. Now for the epiphany: Flags exist between 2 Flag Limits . Easy as that. FL's have edges, and a flag sits right between FL1 for example and FL2. This is stated in the article, I know, but somehow it didn't make me really understand that. It is however, of major importance in reading price.
