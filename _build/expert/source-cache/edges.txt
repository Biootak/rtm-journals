Sometimes also referred to as a kink, FTR or Flag Limit, it represents the decision made at a barrier in the past. For the sake of consistency and to help alleviate any confusion I see it occurring in (3) stages which are outlined below. Stage One - The Kink Price approaches a barrier such as minor Support/Resistance (S/R), Major S/R, Left Shoulder, Ignored barrier or break in/out of Compression (CP) Price pauses and begins a retrace or pull back once the barrier has been broken. It is this hiccup that is now the kink. Stage Two – The FTR If price fails to retrace further and proceeds in the original direction it forms a FTR once price crosses past the origin of the retrace, it has failed to return. At this point a decision has been made by the IT’s to absorb the remaining orders and likely set new orders to be used at a later point. However it is not a confirmed flag limit. Stage Three – The Flag Limit The Flag Limit (FL) is confirmed when an opposing one is hit. Until it is hit the kink and FTR still form part of a flag’s body. This means the FTR and kink can retrace further prior to the opposing FL being hit. Decision Points- Visibility Its also important to understand the best DPs are those ones which are invisible in the higher time frames. You have to remember that a DP could form and be hidden within the range of a candle seconds after it happens. Unless we are there to see it as it occurs we have to keep digging until it is found. Sometimes we will unable to find it so in that case we have to use the whole candle. My favourite is an invisible DP that has a cap form as the first time back which price can fake out to the real DP. Attachments: Check this box to be notified of replies to this topic. Posts: 518 Stage One - The Kink Thank you received: Price approaches a barrier such as minor Support/Resistance (S/R), Major 1373 S/R, Left Shoulder, Ignored barrier or break in/out of Compression (CP) Price pauses and begins a retrace or pull back once the barrier has been broken. It is this hiccup that is now the kink. Stage Two - The FTR If price fails to retrace further and proceeds in the original direction it forms a FTR once price crosses past the origin of the retrace, it has failed to return. At this point a decision has been made by the IT's to absorb the remaining orders and likely set new orders to be used at a later point. However it is not a confirmed flag limit. This means the FTR and kink can retrace further prior to the opposing FL being hit.
