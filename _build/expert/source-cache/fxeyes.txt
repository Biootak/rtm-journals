Go to the right hand side of the swing high/low. Find where price, coming from the H/L, broke the line. Draw LSK from the deepest retrace after the break, back to the furthest advance before the retrace. Should there be smaller kinks in LTF on the right edge of this kink, mark them too, as clean orders here are what allow us to get to the pip entries, right at the LS price.
