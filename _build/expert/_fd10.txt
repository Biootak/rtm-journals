Harry's comment that I posted ended up in my journal because I was confused, so take heart because you are not alone. My (less than) 2 cents worth and suggestions for you: 1. Like Lupy said, treat this like an interest you're investigating right now, as if it were mountain biking, knitting, or photography, but DO NOT worry about what you do or do not understand right now or who is or is not making money - you wouldn't be worried about every detail, method, or maneuver if you were thinking about taking up biking, knitting, or photos, so the same applies here. 2. Forget terminology for now and just think of it this way: price can only go up or down, right? (It never truly goes sideways, so forget that for now.) Yes, price goes up or down. Before it goes up or down, the market makes a decision and that decision is what sends it up or down. Do not allow yourself to complicate it with terms for now! If price went up, the market made a decision. And if price went down, the market made the opposite decision. Nothing more, nothing less. 3. Perhaps you think I'm oversimplifying. OK, then disregard everything that I've said and watch this videoby If and read and reread the following words as many times as necessary until you believe it: "... decisions, that's all supply and demand is and that's all that support and resistance are. It's just decisions on the value of an asset." ~ Ifmyante in the video linked above 4. Once you've heard, read, and watched the video in #3 enough times to believe it like you believe the sun is hot, look at the charts below and keep everything simple and see if you follow as I try to "Find the Decisions" as this thread's title says. I'm trying to A. find an engulf; then B. mark where I think the decision was made to create that engulf; then C. watch what happens the first time back to the price where the decision was made. ABC - we've got this!
