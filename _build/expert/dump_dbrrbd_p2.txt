That's why you wonna find that point and then see how price have reacted to that later, becouse we are dealing whit decision point. Like all in life they have a different value, some are big important point other no etc etc.

Find a drop base rally, mark that area, and see what price have done the next time that price have visited that point and so on. hope that make sense. [sorry for bad eng]
