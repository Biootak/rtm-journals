If every FL Box has an engulf and a fakeout, we can say every FL Box also has an MPL (for a specific scale).

Isn't the market just a collection of decision points that manifest themselves in the form of FL Boxes?

Learning how to trade and mastering the complication of scale is just a matter of understanding how these FL Boxes interact with each other and react off of each other?

If every FO is opposite the EG in a FL Box, and we know the MPL is located at the distal portion of a FTR, we can say the FO that forms the distal portion of a confirmed FL Box is also the MPL and the potential QM level. The FO portion of the first Flag Box has to be FO by a FL Box forming in a higher time frame.

We have to confirm that the FO of the FO is actually a FO...otherwise it may be misinterpreted as an engulf. The only way to confirm a FO of a FO is to

1. See where the FO of the FO is reacting (looking for an important area of liquidity...this is where the 'FO to a different FL Box comes in...) 2. We wait for a completed higher time frame FL box (LL or HH needed to confirm a FL Box)
