WRB: Wide Range Bodies = BIG CANDLES, lots of orders, heavy money making the price move fast and wild.

Hidden gaps: there is no space between the candles, so it is not a true gap. HOWEVER, a WRB candle is called a hidden gap because this very candle must be filled, some people are trapped at the other end of it because it moved too fast. I personnally call them price inconsistencies. The price action is not clean, price has not been retest.

In the way we teach here, hidden gaps are the sources of the poles (you know, poles, flag?). A flag fills the pole and comes back to source. Then the hiddne gap is closed and price can resume in the same direction.

WRB: Wide Range Bodies = کندل‌های بزرگ، سفارش‌های زیاد، پول سنگین که قیمت را سریع و وحشی حرکت می‌دهد.

Hi Marc, What you are asking is really about the basics of supply and demand trading and it's important that you understand it. I wrote an article about Basic Supply & Demand some time ago, you can find it here: www.acegazette.com/en/trading-articles/s...s-levels-by-mel.html

This is important to know that price moves from Supply to Demand and from Demand to Supply. Why does price move from Demand to Supply? Simply because Buyers were in control at this particular point. Why does price move from Supply to Demand? Simply because Sellers were in control at this particular point.

Why is it that price can break Supply and become Demand? Simply because Buyers were in control at this particular point. Why is it that price can break Demand and become Supply? Simply because Sellers were in control at thos particular point.

Our edge is based on this simple facts. When price re-visit a Fresh Demand, we look to buy because there is a good chance that Big Money will have orders placed there and vice versa for a re-vist of a Fresh Supply.
