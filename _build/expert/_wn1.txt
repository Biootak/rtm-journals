##### whynotwork_p1_18 1
Blue is a long FL, red is a short FL, green is a zone to look for a trade, usually a LTF set of orders (the green zone is personal thing of mines, i don't think anyone else uses a green zone). You can see the Blue FLs swap to a Red FL and vice versa, as the FLs are getting broken. Imagine an FL is an area where a transaction is made to hold price up and down. When all the orders in that FL are cleared, the FL gets broken, another transaction is created to hold price the other way. So the FLs swap as they get broken.
