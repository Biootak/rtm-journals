I see youre trying to quantify by what you see, in a measurement perspective.

Understand what length means in the context of S/D trading.

RBD example

the left 'leg' - price has found demand/buyers, and price rises/rallies

the base/top - price has found equilibrium for a relative period of time, but inevitably succumbs to sellers and falls/drops

the right 'leg' - sellers are dropping price, how far depends on price finding a demand that can overcome the sellers.

Now that you are thinking about the ACTUAL MARKET DYNAMICS and not just measuring a leg; if the right leg is in fact longer than the left leg that means the demand that started the whole RBD has been broken and the sellers that dropped that turned price must be pretty powerful. MOMO has been quantified.

SO YES HARSHTHAKR AN RBD WITH A RIGHT LEG LARGER THAN THE LEFT IS SIGNIFICANT AND POWERFUL BUT ALWAYS IN THE CONTEXT OF THE BIG PICTURE.
