Now, I think there are two types of decisions in the market, prime & secondary. The former would be the break of something important like FL, FTR, Swing, SR, SD etc… & the latter the decision to either respect that initial decision (with a FL/FTR) or to reject it (with an Engulf of the primary in the opposite direction). Our job here at RTM is to follow in the footsteps of the last primary decision made until proven wrong (I think!!!), of course while bearing in mind all of what we have so generously been taught i.e. knowing where we are in terms of HTFs S/D and acting in our best interest.
