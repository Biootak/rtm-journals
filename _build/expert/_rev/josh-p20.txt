Hi Josh and Hoeschec, First of all, let me say that you both are doing great! You are learning more and more each day and getting better and better and reading price. In our learning curve, we will come to a time where we are learning quickly, and then come to a halt, and then once we figure it out, we are learning quickly again. Keep on at it and you will start to see which LS to use and which one to ignore.

There is a lot to IF's teaching and it takes time to absorb. As I mentioned to Hoeschec I believe, I had to review video #2 four times before I understood the fake PA in the large kink that broke the support on the Gbp/Cad pair. That part skimmed over my head on the 1st 3 times I watched the video, but on the 4th time, I was looking for an answer to price bouncing off a FL, especially a very large FL and that it when the light bulb went on. Eureka, I understand.

To answer your question and frustration about which LS to use and which one is important, I do have a better understanding now than before when I first took the course. I was frustrated like you, wondering which one will bounce price and which one won't. Here is what I found. We look for LS of a FL that broke another FL to the left, just like a QM. For example, your trend is up and now you form a LS. That LS needs to break a UFL to the left for it to become important. Once we have that, then we marke the high and low of that FL and look for the kink on the right side for PA when price returns to it. Now, we don't know if the kink (on the right side) will hold price or if price will break thru...... hence that is the reason we want to see the the ENG (then the QM/DM) before taking the trade. I find higher time frame LS especially on the Day/Week/Month to be stronger than the lower time frame. The other thing that we must look for in the kink is that have price come up to take the orders in the kink. If it did, then that kink is less likely to hold price. If there are clean orders in the quick, then you are likely to get a bounce. Hopes this helps..

gslao wrote: Hi Josh and Hoeschec, First of all, let me say that you both are doing great! You are learning more and more each day and getting better and better and reading price. In our learning curve, we will come to a time where we are learning quickly, and then come to a halt, and then once we figure it out, we are learning quickly again. Keep on at it and you will start to see which LS to use and which one to ignore.

There is a lot to IF's teaching and it takes time to absorb. As I mentioned to Hoeschec I believe, I had to review video #2 four times before I understood the fake PA in the large kink that broke the support on the Gbp/Cad pair. That part skimmed over my head on the 1st 3 times I watched the video, but on the 4th time, I was looking for an answer to price bouncing off a FL, especially a very large FL and that it when the light bulb went on. Eureka, I understand.

To answer your question and frustration about which LS to use and which one is important, I do have a better understanding now than before when I first took the course. I was frustrated like you, wondering which one will bounce price and which one won't. Here is what I found. We look for LS of a FL that broke another FL to the left, just like a QM. For example, your trend is up and now you form a LS. That LS needs to break a UFL to the left for it to become important. Once we have that, then we marke the high and low of that FL and look for the kink on the right side for PA when price returns to it. Now, we don't know if the kink (on the right side) will hold price or if price will break thru...... hence that is the reason we want to see the the ENG (then the QM/DM) before taking the trade. I find higher time frame LS especially on the Day/Week/Month to be stronger than the lower time frame. The other thing that we must look for in the kink is that have price come up to take the orders in the kink. If it did, then that kink is less likely to hold price. If there are clean orders in the quick, then you are likely to get a bounce. Hopes this helps..

Took me around (10) times watching the vids. Each time something else clicks. We will all get there together. Gregg picked things up a lot quicker than I.
