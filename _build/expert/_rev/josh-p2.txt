I don't know if this will help but I've found the great thing about flags is that so much can be done after the fact. My favorite trades are those that return to a flag after it's been established. So first you can look left to see what the high of the flag is reacting to. Then you can wait for the flag to be fully formed. Then wait for price to leave the flag and then return to it, or not as the case may be (FTR). Even returning to it a second time is often very good. (FTB).

So look to see where price failed to get back into a flag and you can guess there are still sell orders there. Does this make any sense at all?
---FA---
