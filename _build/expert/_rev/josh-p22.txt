Sorry for such a late reply, things have been really hectic lately. For me, decision points are important, but then again they aren't. I've noticed that most decision points are going to give a bounce in price, but FL's are where the real orders lay. Many times, decision points are used to create BSZs. They are normally extremely visible to every trader, and normally happens somewhere near a FL. I very rarely will take a trade from a decision point. When taking trades, only decision points that matter are the ones that occur inside of FLs. For me, decision points are good levels to use for targets.
