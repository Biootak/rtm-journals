Jam,

I don't think IF will mind if I answer some of your questions. I was
actually talking about this with someone else one this forum. Think of
FTR's as the flag limits. I have attached a chart with engulfs and the
following flag limits marked with purple arrows. Notice how price "stair
 steps its way up?" When one of the limits gets engulfed that is your
clue as to where price wants to go! When a whole zone gets engulfed you
will be amazed at how price will respect them on the way down. Notice
how price is compressed while in the zones? I think the trick is to
watch the extremes of these zones and look for PA on smaller time frames
 for confirmation.

Good luck,

H
